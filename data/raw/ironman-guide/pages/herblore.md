# Ironman Herblore Guide

Herblore is one of the hardest skills to train as an ironman - you cannot buy potions or ingredients from the Grand Exchange. Every herb, every secondary, every vial must be self-sourced. This guide covers every method from level 3 to 99: herb farming routes, secondary ingredient sources, quest XP shortcuts, and level-by-level training methods.

## Why Herblore Matters

Despite being self-sourced, Herblore unlocks the most critical consumables in the game. These are the levels that change what content is accessible to you:

| Level | Unlock | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 38 | Prayer potions | Essential for all Prayer-based PvM content |
| 63 | Super restores | Superior to prayer potions; needed for bossing |
| 66 | Super defence | Required component for super combat potions |
| 72 | Ranging potions | Core DPS boost for ranged combat |
| 77 | Stamina potions | Required for efficient raiding (CoX, ToB) and general mobility |
| 81 | Saradomin brews | Primary healing item for endgame PvM |
| 84 | Extended antifire | Dragon tasks become far more manageable |
| 87 | Anti-venom | Required for Zulrah |
| 90 | Super combat potions | Best melee boost; CoX strongest overloads |
| 94 | Anti-venom+ & Extended anti-venom+ | Extended anti-venom+ (araxyte venom sac) is the best venom protection for Zulrah, Araxxor |
| 97 | Divine super combat | Maintains max melee boost for 5 minutes |

### Chambers of Xeric (CoX) Herblore Requirements

CoX overloads are created inside the raid itself (no herbs needed outside). The tier of overload you can make depends on your Herblore level:

- 60 Herblore - Weakest overload
- 75 Herblore - Standard overload
- 90 Herblore - Strongest overload (+6 + 16% to all combat stats, best boost)

The boost refreshes every 15 seconds for 5 minutes, then heals 50 HP. Higher Herblore = better raid performance and team contribution.

Herb farming is the backbone of ironman Herblore. All herb patches have a base growth time of 80 minutes. A full 10-patch run takes 5-10 minutes and yields 50-100+ herbs per circuit.

Maximising Yields

- * Always use **ultracompost** (or cast Fertile Soil with 83 Magic + Lunar spellbook) - minimum 6 herbs per patch, up to 14+
- * Equip **magic secateurs** (from Fairytale I) for a 10% yield boost
- * Note herbs with the tool leprechaun to save inventory space
- * Log out at one of the harder-to-reach patches to start your next run faster

### Kingdom of Miscellania

After completing Throne of Miscellania (and ideally Royal Trouble): assign **10 workers to herbs**, maintain at least **750,000 coins in the coffer** and high approval rating.

- * Yields up to **61 herbs per day** (~427 per week)
- * Herb types: tarromin, harralander, ranarr, irit, avantoe, kwuarm, cadantine, lantadyme, dwarf weed
- * This is a major passive herb source - set it and collect weekly

### Farming Contracts

Requires 45/65/85 Farming for easy/medium/hard contracts. Guildmaster Jane assigns a crop to grow; completing it awards a seed pack.

- * Medium and hard contracts frequently reward **high-value herb seeds** (ranarr, snapdragon, torstol)
- * Keep patches pre-grown to complete contracts quickly back-to-back
- * This is the **single best source of herb seeds** for ironmen

## Training Methods

Ironman Herblore training is entirely secondary-limited. You train when you have herbs and secondaries, and you stockpile when you don't. The goal is to always be making potions you will actually use.

### Levels 1-3: Druidic Ritual

Complete Druidic Ritual (no requirements). Awards 250 XP, unlocking the Herblore skill at level 3.

This is a required quest. There is nothing to make at level 1 - the quest itself is the method.

### Levels 3-26: Quest Your Way Up

Complete these quests in order to skip early training entirely. Total ~9,350 XP, reaching approximately level 26 with zero potion-making. If you cannot complete all quests yet, make attack potions (guam + eye of newt, 25 XP) from 3-12, then strength potions (tarromin + limpwurt root, 50 XP) from 12 onward.

| Potion | Level | Ingredients | XP | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Druidic Ritual (quest) | 1 | - | 250 | Unlocks Herblore at level 3 |
| Jungle Potion (quest) | 3 | - | 775 | Running total: 1,025 (Lv 9) |
| Recruitment Drive (quest) | 1 | - | 1,000 | Requires 12 QP. Running total: 2,025 (Lv 13) |
| Zogre Flesh Eaters (quest) | 8 | - | 2,000 | Requires 30 Ranged, 4 Smithing. Running total: 4,025 (Lv 19) |
| The Dig Site (quest) | 10 | - | 2,000 | Requires 10 Agility, 25 Thieving. Running total: 6,025 (Lv 22) |
| Shades of Mort'ton (quest) | 15 | - | 2,000 | Requires 20 Crafting, 5 Firemaking. Running total: 8,025 (Lv 25) |
| Heroes' Quest (quest) | 25 | - | 1,325 | Requires 55 QP + multiple skills. Running total: 9,350 (Lv 26) |

Fallback if quests are unavailable: Attack potion (Guam unf + Eye of newt, 25 XP) from 3-12; Strength potion (Tarromin unf + Limpwurt root, 50 XP) from 12 onward.

### Levels 26-38: Energy Potions

Energy potions are the most accessible option here. Chocolate bars are bought from shops (no secondary farming required). Need approximately 312 energy potions to reach level 38.

Alternative: Compost potions (22+, harralander + volcanic ash, 60 XP) if you have volcanic ash from Fossil Island.

### Levels 38-45: Prayer Potions

Prayer potions are never wasted on an ironman - you will use every single one you make. Farm ranarrs and collect snape grass from Waterbirth Island (27 spawns) or the hobgoblin peninsula. Need ~356 potions to reach level 45.

| Potion | Level | Ingredients | XP | Notes |

Snape grass sources: Waterbirth Island (27 spawns), hobgoblin peninsula, or farm at 61 Farming.

### Level 77: Stamina Potions

Each stamina(4) needs 4 amylase crystals. An amylase pack (10 marks of grace) gives 100 crystals = 25 stamina(4) potions. If you have been saving marks of grace from Agility training, this is where they pay off. Stamina potions can be tick-manipulated for faster XP (up to ~4,583 potions/hour). Workaround before 77: give a torstol to Dr Jekyll (random event) to receive a stamina potion.

### Useful Tricks & NPCs

- **Dr Jekyll random event** - Always carry a clean torstol. When Dr Jekyll appears, trade him a torstol to receive a stamina potion. Works before level 77 Herblore.
- **Zahur in Nardah** - After the hard Desert Diary (or with a Herblore cape), makes unfinished potions for 200gp each and accepts noted herbs and vials - bulk-create unfinished potions without banking.
- **Wesley in Nardah** - Crushes items (unicorn horns, bird nests, dragon scales, etc.) for 50gp each. Essential for processing secondaries in bulk.
- **Amulet of chemistry** - 5% chance to create a 4-dose potion instead of 3-dose. Saves herbs over time - highly recommended.
- **Prescription goggles** - 10% chance to save secondary ingredients. Obtained from the Mastering Mixology minigame.
- **Herblore cape (99)** - Acts as pestle and mortar; allows making unfinished potions directly from grimy herbs.
- **Making unfinished potions in bulk** - 14 herbs + 14 vials per inventory. Use Zahur (Hard Desert Diary) for noted bulk creation. This separates the herb-collection grind from the secondary-collection grind.
