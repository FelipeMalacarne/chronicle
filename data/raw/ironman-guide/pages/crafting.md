# Ironman Crafting Guide

Complete 1-99 Crafting guide for Ironman accounts. Covers every training method, Golem Crafting on Wyrmscraig, the Superglass Make meta, quest XP routing, and every milestone from tiara to Amulet of Torture. All level requirements and XP values verified against the OSRS Wiki as of September 2026.

## Why Crafting Matters for Ironmen

Crafting is one of the most important skills for Ironman accounts because it gates access to best-in-slot jewelry that cannot be traded on the Grand Exchange. From early tiaras for Runecrafting to endgame Zenyte jewelry, your Crafting level directly controls the quality of gear you can equip.

### Levels 60-99: Final Stretch

**Golem Crafting (60+):** the Wyrmscraig method added in July 2026. The wiki puts it at roughly 100,000 XP/hr at a relaxed pace with island goat fur and upwards of 185,000 XP/hr at maximum efficiency, with no Magic requirement, no Lunar Diplomacy, and no seaweed. Needs Fallen From Grace (62 Sailing) and one Hunter fur per golem. See the dedicated section below.

**Black d'hide bodies (84+):** 258 XP each, ~435,000-460,000 XP/hr. Requires large quantities of black dragonhide - Vorkath is the best source.

**Superglass Make + glassblowing (61+):** Self-sufficient, produces useful items, and grants Magic XP alongside Crafting. Still the pick if you want vials, orbs, and light orbs out of your training, but since Wyrmscraig it is no longer the only supply-free route. See the dedicated section below.

**Dorgesh-Kaan lamp fixing (87+):** ~130,000 Crafting XP/hr plus bonus Firemaking and Thieving XP. Requires The Lost Tribe quest, 87 Crafting (to make light orbs yourself), and 52 Firemaking.

## The Superglass Make Method

The single most important Crafting training method for Ironman accounts. It is self-sufficient, produces useful items, and grants Magic XP simultaneously.

### Requirements

- 77 Magic (boostable)
- 61 Crafting (Lunar Diplomacy quest requirement, unboostable)
- 23 Farming (for giant seaweed patches)
- Lunar Diplomacy quest completed

### How It Works

Superglass Make converts buckets of sand + seaweed/soda ash into molten glass **without a furnace**. The spell produces **bonus glass** beyond what the raw materials would normally yield - this is what makes it so powerful.

Spell cost per cast: 10 Air runes + 6 Fire runes + 2 Astral runes (~290 gp)

Magic XP per cast: 78

Crafting XP per cast: 10 XP per bucket of sand used

### Bonus Glass Production

Extra molten glass appears on the ground when your inventory is full. Picking it up maximises your yield. The best setup uses giant seaweed with pickup.

| Setup | Materials per cast | Glass per sand | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard seaweed | 1:1 ratio | ~1.3x | Less efficient |
| Giant seaweed (no pickup) | 3 giant seaweed + 18 sand | ~1.45x | Faster but less glass |
| Giant seaweed (with pickup) | 3 giant seaweed + 18 sand | ~1.6x | Pick up overflow - best yield |

### Step 1: Gathering Giant Seaweed

Farm underwater seaweed patches on Fossil Island (accessible by diving from Fossil Island). Requires 23 Farming. Growth time: ~40 minutes.

- Yield per patch with ultracompost: 15-34 giant seaweed
- Yield with ultracompost + Attas seed (76 Farming): 44+ giant seaweed
- Magic secateurs do **not** affect yield
- Typical run across all patches with ultracompost: ~50 giant seaweed

Seaweed spore sources: random spawns on the seabed while diving, Lobstrosities, ammonite crabs, and Mairin's Market (20 mermaid's tears each).

### Step 2: Gathering Buckets of Sand

Best method: mine sandstone at the Quarry (Desert, west of Bandit Camp), grind it at the Sandstorm grinder, and collect noted sand from Drew. Drew stores up to 25,000 empty buckets and 25,000 buckets of sand. Collection costs 50 gp per bucket.

| Sandstone size | Sand yield |
| --- | --- |
| 1 kg | 1 bucket |
| 2 kg | 2 buckets |
| 5 kg | 4 buckets |
| 10 kg | 8 buckets |

Mining rate: ~2,000-3,500 buckets/hr normal; ~4,000-6,000 buckets/hr with tick manipulation.

Alternative: **Bert** in Yanille gives 84 free buckets of sand daily after The Hand in the Sand quest (49 Crafting, 17 Thieving).

### Step 3: Casting Superglass Make

Optimal inventory: 3 giant seaweed + 18 buckets of sand + runes. Use a staff of air or smoke battlestaff to free up rune slots.

1. Withdraw 3 giant seaweed and 18 buckets of sand
2. Cast Superglass Make
3. Pick up any overflow molten glass from the ground
4. Bank and repeat

### Step 4: Blowing the Glass

Use a glassblowing pipe on molten glass to produce glass items. The item you blow determines your Crafting XP.

| Glass Item | Level | XP | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beer glass | 1 | 17.5 | - |
| Empty candle lantern | 4 | 19 | - |
| Empty oil lamp | 12 | 25 | - |
| Vial | 33 | 35 | Useful for Herblore |
| Unpowered orb | 46 | 52.5 | Charge into orbs for battlestaves |
| Lantern lens | 49 | 55 | Good XP; sells to shops or alch |
| Empty light orb | 87 | 70 | Best XP per glass; used for Dorgesh-Kaan lamps |

Recommended blowing order:

33-46: Vials - you will need thousands for Herblore

46-49: Unpowered orbs - useful for battlestaves and orb charging

49-87: Lantern lenses - best XP until 87; can sell to general store

87+: Empty light orbs - 70 XP each; used in Dorgesh-Kaan lamp fixing

## Golem Crafting (Wyrmscraig)

Added on 29 July 2026 with Wyrmscraig. The fastest Crafting XP available to an ironman without Magic, Lunar Diplomacy, or farmed seaweed: 60 Crafting and one Hunter fur per golem. Figures below are from the OSRS Wiki as of September 2026 unless labelled otherwise.

### Requirements

- 62 Sailing to reach Wyrmscraig (not boostable)
- Q Fallen From Grace: 62 Sailing, 60 Crafting, 53 Mining, 47 Runecraft, and Pandemonium
- 60 Crafting for the activity itself
- 48 Construction to build the island bank chest (optional, but it makes the island self-contained)
- -- Chisel, hammer (the Imcando hammer works off-hand), pickaxe, and a fur pouch

### How It Works

1. Mine sunstone in the Wyrmscraig mine. Mining individual rocks builds momentum, which raises your success chance and gives a chance at a second sunstone when you move to a new rock within 5 ticks. The sunstone monolith is the AFK option, at a lower rate.
2. Click an empty plinth with a chisel, a hammer, and 4 sunstones to start a golem.
3. Carve all four sides. Each side takes 20 ticks (80 total, plus movement), or 15 ticks (60 total) if you click on the tick of each XP drop or spam-click. Carved sides leave debris on the ground, so you can see which are done.
4. Use a chisel on a sunstone to make a sunstone core, then insert the core together with one Hunter fur. That completes the golem for the Crafting XP and an uncut gem.

The fur is mandatory. Tatty furs and combat-drop furs do not work, and furs are used straight from a fur pouch.

### XP per Golem by Fur

Every golem gives a base of 1,560 Crafting XP plus the fur's value. All furs work at 60 Crafting; the Hunter level only matters if you catch them yourself.

Golem Crafting beats every glassblowing method (empty light orbs top out around 122,500 XP/hr and need 87 Crafting) and overlaps with Superglass Make on raw rate. Its edge is that nothing gates it except the quest and a fur supply.

### The No-Banking Rotation

Sunstone is untradeable, does not stack, and weighs 2.5 kg each. Every golem uses 5 (4 for the base, 1 for the core). Carry a large fur pouch with at least 25 furs, an empty gem bag, and a chisel, and keep 25 inventory slots free. Then:

1. Mine 25 sunstone.
2. Chisel 5 of them into sunstone cores.
3. Craft 5 golems. Since 12 August 2026 the gems go straight into your gem bag.
4. Return to the rocks and repeat.

A full large fur pouch lasts five rotations (25 golems) before you need to bank. Start carving on the east or west side and leave north for last so you can chisel the core and insert it from the same tile.

### Recommended Gear

- Rune pickaxe if you are actively mining for momentum (the wiki notes momentum fails often with higher-tier pickaxes); otherwise your best pickaxe
- Imcando hammer (off-hand), so it sits beside the pickaxe and frees an inventory slot
- Expert mining gloves and Varrock armour for extra sunstone
- Prospector outfit, plus a charged amulet of glory for better gem rolls from sunstone rocks
- Large fur pouch, gem bag, chisel

### Rewards and the Jeweller's Chisel

Each completed golem gives one uncut gem: sapphire 15/23, emerald 5/23, ruby 2/23, diamond 1/23. All of them are more Crafting XP once cut.

The unique is the Jeweller's chisel at 1/300 (confirmed by Jagex on 12 August 2026). It is untradeable and works as a normal chisel with three extras: +20% success chance when cutting opals, jades, and red topaz; guaranteed success on opals at 62, jades at 71, and red topaz at 76 Crafting; and a 10% chance to cut two gems at once from any gem, including dragonstones and zenytes.

### Golem Crafting vs Superglass Make

|  | Golem Crafting | Superglass Make |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crafting level | 60 | 61 (Lunar Diplomacy, unboostable) |
| Other requirements | Fallen From Grace (62 Sailing, 53 Mining, 47 Runecraft, Pandemonium) | 77 Magic, Lunar Diplomacy, 23 Farming for seaweed |
| Supplies per action | 5 sunstone + 1 Hunter fur per golem, all on-island or from falconry | 3 giant seaweed + 18 buckets of sand + astral/air/fire runes per cast |
| Crafting XP/hr (wiki) | ~100,000 relaxed to 185,000+ at max efficiency | 108,000-153,000 optimised |
| Side XP | ~28,000 Hunter/hr if using island goats | 46,800-66,300 Magic/hr |
| Products | Uncut gems, Jeweller's chisel (1/300) | Vials, orbs, lantern lenses, light orbs |

Pick Golem Crafting when the goal is Crafting levels with the least setup. Pick Superglass Make when you also want the Magic XP and the glass products. The island, the quest, and Goat Hunting are covered in full in the [Wyrmscraig ironman guide](https://ironman.guide/articles/wyrmscraig-ironman-guide).

## Ironman Tips

### Daily Habits

- **Buy battlestaves from Zaff every day.** Even before you can attach orbs, stockpile them. With Varrock Elite diary that is 120 battlestaves/day (840,000 gp cost); alching them as air battlestaves at 9,300 gp each returns ~276,000 gp profit, or ~250,000-260,000 gp after nature runes.
- **Claim 84 buckets of sand from Bert daily** after completing The Hand in the Sand (49 Crafting, 17 Thieving).
- **Do seaweed farming runs** whenever you pass Fossil Island. Each run takes 2-3 minutes and yields ~50 giant seaweed with ultracompost.

### When to Start Giant Seaweed Runs

- Start at **23 Farming** - the sooner the better.
- Even before 77 Magic, stockpile giant seaweed for later.
- You need 3 giant seaweed per bank trip - Superglass Make is one cast per inventory.
- Stockpiling early means you can blitz Crafting levels the moment you unlock the spell.

### Glass Item Priorities

1. **Vials (33 Crafting):** Make thousands - you will need them all for Herblore.
2. **Unpowered orbs (46 Crafting):** Charge at the elemental obelisks (air/earth: Wilderness; water/fire: Taverley Dungeon), attach to battlestaves, alch for money.
3. **Lantern lenses (49 Crafting):** Best XP per glass until 87; sell to general store or drop.
4. **Empty light orbs (87 Crafting):** Best XP per glass; use for Dorgesh-Kaan lamp fixing.

### Optimal Gem Cutting Order

Cut gems as soon as you obtain them - each type is directly useful:

1. **Sapphires:** Games necklaces (Barbarian Outpost, Wintertodt), rings of recoil
2. **Emeralds:** Rings of dueling (Ferox Enclave bank, PvP Arena, Castle Wars), necklaces of passage
3. **Rubies:** Rings of forging (100% iron smelting - not needed at Blast Furnace, which is already 100%), ruby bolts (e)
4. **Diamonds:** Rings of life, diamond bolts (e)
5. **Dragonstones:** Amulets of glory, combat bracelets, rings of wealth
