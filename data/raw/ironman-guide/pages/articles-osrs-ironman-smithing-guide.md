# OSRS Ironman Smithing Training Guide 2026: 1-99 Methods, Ore Sources & Bar Budget

Ironman Smithing 1-99 in OSRS: quests to 40, Blast Furnace gold, Giants' Foundry, every ore and coal source, cannonballs, dart tips and your bar budget.

26 min read·Updated October 22, 2026·Difficulty: **

Smithing on an ironman comes down to ore before anything else. A main buys bars by the thousand. You mine every ore yourself, take it off a monster, or buy it from one of a handful of shops with coins you earned. That shapes the whole guide: gold ore at the Blast Furnace turns coins straight into XP, while every iron, mithril, adamant and rune bar you own is a supply you will want later for cannonballs, darts and your boat.

This guide follows the OSRS Wiki's Ironman Guide for Smithing and its Smithing training page, checked as of October 2026, including the runite golem changes from 14 October. On top of that it shows where each input comes from.

The three rules of ironman Smithing

1. **Quest to 40.** Eight quests carry you from 1 to 40 Smithing, and the best training method in the skill starts at 40.
2. **Buy your XP with coins, not bars.** Gold ore from Ordan at the Blast Furnace is the fastest XP in the skill, and gold bars have few other uses. Every coal-based bar is a supply.
3. **When you do spend bars on XP, spend them at the Giants' Foundry.** The Ironman Guide puts it at roughly ten times the XP per bar of anvil smithing. It pays coins, and it sells the double ammo mould and the Smiths' Uniform.

## Levels 1-40: Quests

Done in this order, every Smithing requirement is already met when you reach it. The Knight's Sword alone takes a level 1 character to 29.

Level 40 is 37,224 XP, so all eight finish the job. Without Heroes' Quest you stop at 35,725, level 39, about 1,500 XP short. The Forsaken Tower adds another 500.

The two iron bars for The Knight's Sword are the first ironman puzzle. You can smelt them at 15 Mining and 15 Smithing. You can also pick up the spawn in level 18 Wilderness north-west of the Ferox Enclave, or the safer one in Thurid's Brain Buckets on the Sunset Coast in Varlamore after Children of the Sun. Dwarves under Falador drop one at 1/42.67, and sorting small salvage at 15 Sailing gives one at 1/34.42.

The Tourist Trap needs 20 Smithing and rewards two 4,650 XP choices from Agility, Fletching, Smithing and Thieving, so up to 9,300 Smithing if you want it. Most ironmen spend that XP elsewhere. The real reward is the ability to smith dart tips.

Later quests that reward Smithing XP, per the wiki: Scrambled! (5,000, 35 Smithing), The Red Reef (5,000, 48 Smithing and 52 Sailing), Between a Rock... (5,000, 50), Cabin Fever (7,000, 50), Devious Minds (6,500, 65), Defender of Varrock (15,000, 55), Song of the Elves (40,000, 70) and Dragon Slayer II (80,000, 70). All the set quest rewards together come to 202,000 XP. Song of the Elves and Dragon Slayer II alone are 120,000, and both need a real, unboostable 70. Our [quest order](https://ironman.guide/quests) shows where they fall.

## Levels 40-99: Blast Furnace Gold

The wiki's verdict for ironmen is the same as for mains: from 40 onwards the fastest Smithing is smelting gold ore bought from Ordan at the Blast Furnace while wearing goldsmith gauntlets.

**What you need:**

- **Keldagrim access.** Start The Giant Dwarf by talking to the Dwarven Boatman east of Rellekka. After that, the Minigame Teleport drops you outside the Blast Furnace.
- **Goldsmith gauntlets** from Family Crest (40 Mining, 40 Smithing, 40 Crafting and 59 Magic, all boostable). They raise a gold bar from 22.5 to 56.2 XP. Avan in the Al Kharid mine enchants the steel gauntlets you get from the quest. If you also want the chaos or cooking gauntlets, the Family Pest miniquest (500,000 coins) lets you claim all three from Avan at any time.
- **Ice gloves** from the Ice Queen under White Wolf Mountain, so you can take bars straight from the dispenser. Reaching her needs 50 Mining (boostable) to clear a rock obstacle, but the wiki notes the gloves are obtainable at combat level 3. Smiths gloves (i) do the same job.
- **Coins.** On the official Blast Furnace worlds the dwarves cost 72,000 coins an hour, paid into the coffer at 12 coins a tick and drained only while you are in the furnace room. Below 60 Smithing, which cannot be boosted, the foreman also wants 2,500 coins every 10 minutes, or 1,250 with a Ring of Charos(a).

**The rates.** The training page lists about 380,000 XP per hour with the gauntlets, 164,000 without them and 410,000 with the Smithing cape, rising to 390,000 and 420,000 with consistent 0-tick banking. Those figures assume the ore is already in your bank. Buying from Ordan as you go means hopping worlds for stock, and the Ironman Guide puts that at more than 230,000 XP an hour. If you buy the ore in a separate session first, it puts the realistic buying pace at 8,000 to 9,000 gold ore an hour, three inventories per world.

**The cost.** The Ironman Guide prices Ordan's gold ore at 225 coins, or about 4 coins per XP before the furnace fee. The training page counts 231,267 gold ore from 40 to 99, which at 225 coins each is roughly 52 million coins in ore alone. For ways to fund it, see the [money making guide](https://ironman.guide/money-making).

**What to do with the gold bars.** The Ironman Guide notes they have limited uses, which is exactly why gold is the metal to spend on levels. Make gold jewellery for [Crafting](https://ironman.guide/crafting), or alch it: the Ironman Guide's own cost table treats gold bracelets plus High Level Alchemy as the way to claw some coins back.

Run energy at the furnace

The wiki's rates assume stamina potions, which need 77 Herblore to make, so expect slower rates before that. A ring of endurance from the Hallowed Sepulchre (70 Agility) cuts run energy drain by 15% once it holds 500 or more charges, and doubles the effect of each stamina dose you drink while wearing it.

### Other bars at the Blast Furnace

The Blast Furnace needs half the coal of a normal furnace and always succeeds with iron ore, which fails 50% of the time at a normal furnace without a ring of forging. The coal bag holds 27 coal (36 with the Smithing cape equipped). Rates from the Blast Furnace page, wearing ice gloves:

| Bar | Level | XP per bar | XP/hour | XP/hour with coal bag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Iron | 15 | 12.5 | 72,500-85,600 | No change |
| Steel | 30 | 17.5 | 50,800-59,500 | 81,400-95,400 |
| Gold (goldsmith gauntlets) | 40 | 56.2 | 314,700-370,900 | No change |
| Mithril | 50 | 30 | 57,000-67,500 | 94,500-111,000 |
| Adamantite | 70 | 37.5 | 54,400-63,800 | 90,000-105,000 |
| Runite | 85 | 50 | 57,500-67,500 | 95,000-112,500 |

The hybrid patterns, which mix gold ore in with a coal bag, are worth knowing if you are smelting ore you mined anyway: mithril and gold for 191,800-226,300 XP an hour, adamantite and gold for 221,100-262,300, and runite and gold for 240,500-284,200.

Ordan does not sell adamantite or runite ore, only copper, tin, iron, mithril, silver, gold and coal. The Ironman Guide prices iron ore at 25 coins, coal at 67 and mithril ore at 243. Its own example: 200,000 coins buys around 4,000 iron ore or 1,400 coal, which it argues beats mining them yourself if you earn coins faster than you would mine.

## Levels 15-99: The Giants' Foundry

The Giants' Foundry needs Sleeping Giants. Kovac commissions a giant sword. You set three mould parts, fill the crucible with 28 bars' worth of metal, pour, and then work the preform through the trip hammer, grindstone and polishing wheel at the right temperatures. A finished sword pays Smithing XP, coins straight into your bank and Foundry reputation. Use a two-metal alloy, because mixed metals score far higher than one metal.

For an ironman it solves two problems. It gets the most XP out of every bar you have to spend, and it does not have to use your bars at all. Smithed equipment counts as the number of bars it took to make minus one, so a warhammer counts as 2 bars and a platebody as 4.

The Ironman Guide's per-bar figures, default moulds against the best moulds, without the Smiths' Uniform:

| Alloy (14:14) | Level | XP per bar | XP/hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze/iron | 15 | 143.6-218.6 | 56,290-85,690 |
| Iron/steel | 30 | 215.4-303.2 | 84,430-118,850 |
| Steel/mithril | 50 | 321.4-425.4 | 116,980-154,840 |
| Mithril/adamantite | 70 | 473.6-595.7 | 145,860-183,470 |
| Adamantite/runite | 85 | 684.6-828.2 | 172,520-208,700 |

Compare that with 12.5 to 75 XP per bar at an anvil. The training page's optimal figures (all optimal moulds, which need up to 81 Smithing) also list the coins: 329,280 coins an hour from Kovac for steel/mithril and 1,722 reputation an hour, up to 506,220 coins and 2,068 reputation for adamantite/runite.

**Feeding the crucible without your own bars.** The Ironman Guide and its Ultimate Ironman counterpart name the shops:

- **Warhammers** , 2 bars each and among the cheapest shop sources at every tier: the Cam Torum Blacksmith (Perilous Moons started, highest stock, near a bank), Vigr's Warhammers, and Skulgrimen's Battle Gear.
- **Platebodies, platelegs and plateskirts:** the Fortis Blacksmith in Civitas illa Fortis stocks them up to adamant after Children of the Sun. Salius' Armour Shop in Salvager Overlook has the most platebodies but needs the Landing Site built for fast travel. Ranael's and Louie's in Al Kharid sell skirts and legs. The UIM guide estimates levels 30 to 70 this way at under 500,000 coins.
- **Mithril claws** from Jatizso's weapon shop are cheap filler for a steel/mithril alloy.
- **Aneirin's Armour** in Prifddinas, after Song of the Elves, sells mithril and adamant armour. The Ironman Guide calls it the best Foundry XP from 70, though not a profitable one.

One warning from the wiki: do not smith your own bars into items and then melt them, because you get less XP per bar than pouring the bars in directly. Buying premade items is different. The Ironman Guide notes that steel warhammers and mithril claws from shops make a steel/mithril sword that always pays back more than the items cost.

## The Smiths' Uniform

Four pieces from the Giants' Foundry Reward Shop: the tunic and trousers for 4,000 reputation each, and the gloves and boots for 3,500 each, so 15,000 in total. Kovac buys them back at 80%. The bonus is the same on every piece, so the wiki suggests buying the cheaper ones first.

- **At an anvil** , each piece gives a 20% chance to make an action take 4 ticks instead of 5, and the full set makes it every action. The training page puts the gain at 15% to 25% depending on what you smith, and its dart tip rates rise from 1,060 bars an hour to 1,300.
- **At the Foundry** , each piece gives a 20% chance of extra progress on the preform, and the full set guarantees it. The strategy page puts the effective gain at about 15%. The wiki's pages disagree on this number, quoting anywhere from 5% to 20%.
- **Smiths gloves plus ice gloves** combine into smiths gloves (i), which does both jobs, and you can split them again.

## Superheat Item

At 43 Magic, Superheat Item smelts one ore in your inventory for 1 nature rune and 4 fire runes, castable every 3 ticks. It gives 53 Magic XP and the normal Smithing XP for the bar. It always succeeds on iron ore, makes steel if you carry two coal, draws from your coal bag, and the goldsmith gauntlets, Smithing cape and smithing catalyst all work with it.

The Ironman Guide's early use for it is iron dart tips. Mine iron ore in the south-east Ardougne mine, superheat it while you walk to the East Ardougne bank, then teleport back with the Ardougne cloak. Bank enough bars to carry Fletching to about 50-55, and the darts double as early Ranged ammo and Slayer luring. The cost is a nature rune per bar, so use it on ore you mined anyway. The [Magic guide](https://ironman.guide/articles/osrs-ironman-magic-guide) covers where the nature runes come from.

## Ore and Coal Sources

### Ordan at the Blast Furnace

Ordan's prices are fixed for every account type and do not rise as stock falls. Each ore has a normal stock of 100, and a dwarf restocks one random ore type every 27 seconds. Stock is shared, so expect to hop worlds. This is the core of ironman Smithing: copper, tin, iron, mithril, silver and gold ore and coal for coins.

### Motherlode Mine and the coal bag

The Motherlode Mine needs 30 Mining (unboostable). Pay-dirt gives coal, gold, mithril, adamantite and runite ore as your Mining level allows, and each pay-dirt has a 3.13% chance of a golden nugget, about one per 32 pay-dirt. For Smithing, the reward that matters is the **coal bag**, 100 nuggets from Prospector Percy. The wiki says it greatly affects Blast Furnace rates for any bar that needs coal, and you can only own one. The upper level needs 57 Mining and a one-time 100 nuggets.

### Blast Mine

The Blast Mine in Lovakengj needs 43 Mining, a chisel, a tinderbox and dynamite, and treats your Mining level as 10 higher, so runite ore drops from 75. You collect the ore noted from the operator. At 99 Mining with the elite Kourend & Kebos Diary and a celestial ring, the wiki's distribution is 38.6% adamantite, 31.4% mithril, 14.3% gold, 11.2% runite and 4.5% coal. Thirus sells dynamite, and gives 20, 40 or 80 free each day with the medium, hard or elite Kourend & Kebos Diary.

### Volcanic Mine

On Fossil Island (Bone Voyage), with 50 Mining and 30 numulite per entry or 3,000 for permanent access. Points buy ore from Petrified Pete: 60 points per coal, 150 per gold or mithril ore, 300 per adamantite ore and 855 per runite ore. The solo vent-capping method the wiki describes earns about 13,600 points an hour. The wiki flags the activity as dangerous: the volcano erupts after ten minutes and kills anyone still inside.

### Miscellania coal

Managing Miscellania (after Throne of Miscellania) can put up to 10 workers on coal. With Royal Trouble done, 10 workers and at least 750,000 coins in the coffers, the wiki's daily maximum is 546 coal plus some uncut gems, for up to 75,000 coins a day in wages. On 14 October 2026 Jagex adjusted the bonus-resource rounding in Kingdom Management so it hands out more bonus resources. Resources go straight to your bank, so ultimate ironmen cannot use it (see the [UIM guide](https://ironman.guide/uim)).

### Runite golems (changed 14 October 2026)

Two runite golems live in the Wilderness Resource Area east of the Deserted Keep. Kill one and its head becomes a runite rock you can mine with 85 Mining for one minute. The golem respawns five minutes later. Entry costs 7,500 coins, 6,000 with the medium Wilderness Diary, 3,750 with the hard and nothing with the elite. Inside are a furnace, an anvil and Piles, who notes items for 50 coins each.

The **More Gathering Efficiencies** update on 14 October 2026 changed three things:

1. A higher chance to get at least one runite ore from the rock before the golem respawns.
2. A chance of extra ore on each successful mine: up to 2 total with the medium Wilderness Diary, 3 with hard and 4 with elite.
3. Expert mining gloves now work on the rock. They can stop it depleting once, but they do not stop the golem respawning.

Varrock armour 4 still gives a 10% chance to double the ore, so the wiki notes up to 8 runite ore from a single action with the elite diary.

The golem has 120 Hitpoints, a 60% weakness to earth spells since June 2025, a Magic level of 1 and no magic defence bonus. Your Mining level scales your melee accuracy and max hit against it, while Ranged and Magic use your normal stats. A second hit landing after it dies, such as a dark bow's second arrow, despawns the rock.

Hardcore ironmen: this is the Wilderness

The Resource Area is deep Wilderness and the wiki warns that players can attack you there. Logging out puts you outside the gate, and you pay the fee again to re-enter. The other Wilderness ore stop on an ironman's list is the level 18 iron bar spawn for The Knight's Sword, and the Ironman Guide points hardcores to the Sunset Coast spawn instead. See the [HCIM guide](https://ironman.guide/hcim).

The same update announced an in-game Mining & Smithing survey for a future "Mining & Smithing: Reforged". Nothing in it is decided, and nothing in this guide changes because of it.

### Zalcano

Zalcano under Prifddinas needs Song of the Elves, which means at least 70 Mining and Smithing, and pays about 3,000 Smithing XP an hour while you fight. Her loot is the reason to go. Each of these noted drops has the rate shown: coal 169-1,052 (1/36), gold ore 86-756 (3/36), mithril ore 56-469 (2/36), adamantite ore 51-343 (2/36), runite ore 3-31 (2/36), steel bars 16-699 (3/36), mithril bars 53-501 (3/36), adamantite bars 13-111 (2/36) and runite bars 3-27 (2/36). The Ironman Guide suggests putting the bars into the Foundry, platebodies for alching, or dart tips for the toxic blowpipe.

## Cannonballs

Steel cannonballs need Dwarf Cannon and 35 Smithing. A normal ammo mould makes 4 per steel bar for 25.6 XP a bar. The Ironman Guide's rates at the Edgeville furnace are 2,160 cannonballs and 13,824 XP an hour with a normal mould, or 4,320 and 27,648 with the **double ammo mould**. That makes the mould the first Giants' Foundry purchase for anyone who cannons.

With 87 Sailing, the **Ancient Furnace** in the Grimstone Dungeon makes cannonballs in half the time. The fairy ring there (DLP) works once you have moored at Grimstone. With a double mould the wiki expects about 1,500 bars an hour, or 6,000 cannonballs, banking by teleport. If you bring Isles from Port Roberts to unnote bars beside the furnace for 100 coins a bar, it rises to about 2,150 bars and 8,600 cannonballs.

- **Granite cannonballs** (50 Smithing, no XP) are a steel cannonball plus granite dust, and raise the dwarf multicannon's max hit from 30 to 35. The dust comes from the Grotesque Guardians at 75 Slayer, or from the Mad Angel on Wyrmscraig, who drops 25-35 every kill and steel cannonballs (90-100) at 16/150.
- **On land the dwarf multicannon fires only steel and granite cannonballs.** The other metals are for boat cannons: bronze (5 Smithing), iron (20), steel (35), mithril (55), adamant (75) and rune (90), with dragon cannonballs drop-only. Iron Bill's stall in Port Roberts keeps bronze, iron and mithril cannonballs in stock, but its steel, adamant and rune stock starts at zero. A ballistic attractor recovers 60% of the cannonballs your boat fires.

## The Ironman Bar Budget

Gold is XP. Everything else is a supply with a queue of uses. Before you put coal-based bars into platebodies or a Foundry sword, check what they are already promised to:

- **Cannonballs.** The Ironman Guide estimates about 35,000 steel cannonballs to take Ranged from 40 to 70 with a cannon, which is 8,750 steel bars, and Slayer burns more on top.
- **Dart tips** for Fletching and the toxic blowpipe, from mithril upwards.
- **Sailing.** Hulls, helms, keels, salvaging hooks and boat cannonballs all eat bars. The adamant keel alone is 50 adamantite bars at 74 Smithing. The steel-and-up hooks add lead bars, and the rune and dragon tiers need cupronickel bars. The Wyrmscraig bank chest takes 2 lead bars and 32 mithril nails, and each Deepfin Mine bank chest takes 2 cupronickel and 2 adamantite bars.
- **Utility.** A mith grapple tip (59 Smithing) for the grapple shortcuts and The Curse of Arrav. The wiki lists 59 Smithing and 59 Fletching as an ironman requirement there, unless you get the grapple as a drop in the Ancient Cavern. Construction nails, and the steel bars Mahogany Homes contracts use.
- **Quests.** The Knight's Sword needs its 2 iron bars, and several quests ask for bars along the way.

Mithril and adamantite bars from Slayer drops and Zalcano are the ones most worth spending on XP, and the place to spend them is the Foundry.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best way to train Smithing as an ironman in OSRS?

Quest to 40 first: The Knight's Sword, Sleeping Giants, both Elemental Workshops, Prying Times, The Giant Dwarf, the Pirate Pete part of Recipe for Disaster and Heroes' Quest give 38,000 Smithing XP between them, which is level 40. From there, smelt gold ore bought from Ordan at the Blast Furnace while wearing the goldsmith gauntlets from Family Crest, which raise each gold bar from 22.5 to 56.2 XP. The wiki's training page lists about 380,000 XP per hour with the ore already in your bank; buying from Ordan as you go, its Ironman Guide puts the rate at more than 230,000. It costs about 4 coins per XP plus the furnace fee. The Giants' Foundry is the slower, cheaper alternative.

### Where do ironmen get ore and coal for Smithing?

Ordan at the Blast Furnace sells copper, tin, iron, mithril, silver and gold ore and coal at fixed prices. Beyond that: pay-dirt from the Motherlode Mine (which also sells the coal bag), the Blast Mine in Lovakengj, Petrified Pete's ore shop at the Volcanic Mine, up to 546 coal a day from Managing Miscellania, Zalcano's noted ore and bar drops, Slayer drops such as gargoyles, wyverns, dust devils and lizardman shamans, runite golems in the Wilderness Resource Area, and the Sailing islands, including Wyrmscraig, Deepfin Mine, Ynysdail and Grimstone.

### Is the Giants' Foundry worth it for ironmen?

Yes, whenever you have to spend metal on XP. The wiki's Ironman Guide puts Foundry swords at roughly ten times the XP per bar of anvil smithing: 143.6 to 828.2 XP per bar depending on the alloy and moulds, against 12.5 to 75 XP per bar at an anvil. Kovac also pays coins for every sword, and the reward shop sells the double ammo mould, the Smiths' Uniform, smithing catalysts and Kovac's grog. Shop-bought armour and warhammers count towards the crucible, so you do not have to use your own bars.

### What quests give Smithing XP in OSRS?

Per the OSRS Wiki: Dragon Slayer II (80,000), Song of the Elves (40,000), Defender of Varrock (15,000), The Knight's Sword (12,725), Elemental Workshop II (7,500), Cabin Fever (7,000), Devious Minds (6,500), Sleeping Giants (6,000), Elemental Workshop I, Between a Rock..., Scrambled! and The Red Reef (5,000 each), The Giant Dwarf (2,500), Heroes' Quest (2,275), Prying Times and Freeing Pirate Pete (1,000 each) and The Forsaken Tower (500), for 202,000 in total. The Tourist Trap also lets you put two 4,650 XP rewards into Smithing.

### How much does the Blast Furnace cost per hour?

On the official Blast Furnace worlds you pay the dwarves 72,000 coins per hour through the coffer, which only drains while you are in the furnace room. Below 60 Smithing, which cannot be boosted, the foreman also charges 2,500 coins every 10 minutes, or 1,250 with a Ring of Charos(a). The ore is the real cost: the wiki's Ironman Guide prices Ordan's gold ore at 225 coins, which works out to about 4 coins per XP.

### What should an ironman save bars for?

Steel bars for cannonballs (the wiki's Ironman Guide estimates about 35,000 steel cannonballs, or 8,750 steel bars, to take Ranged from 40 to 70 with a cannon), mithril and higher bars for dart tips for Fletching and the blowpipe, nails for Construction, and Sailing: boat parts, salvaging hooks and cannonballs all take bars, the adamant keel alone needs 50 adamantite bars, and the higher boat tiers need lead and cupronickel bars. Gold is the one metal to spend freely on XP.

### What changed for runite golems in October 2026?

The More Gathering Efficiencies update on 14 October 2026 raised the chance of getting at least one runite ore from a killed golem before it respawns, added a chance of extra ore per successful mine based on the Wilderness Diary (up to 2 total with the medium diary, 3 with hard and 4 with elite), and made expert mining gloves work on the golem's rock. Varrock armour 4 can still double the ore, so up to 8 runite ore can come from one action with the elite diary. The golems are still in the Wilderness Resource Area, with its 7,500-coin entry fee before diary discounts.
