About Us

What Is TempleOSRS?
TempleOSRS is an Old School Runescape tracker developed to provide experience and kill count tracking for accounts in Old School Runescape.

Our website is developed and curated by Mikael, with support from a handful of other volunteers.

Feel free to follow our Twitter and join our Discord server for the latest site updates!
How Does TempleOSRS Work?
TempleOSRS works by creating a snapshot of your account's official hiscores page when you press the "Update player" button on your profile. This information, alongside a timestamp, is stored in a database on our website to provide the core data for most of our features. Our website will then compare the earliest and latest data points within the period specified on your player page (default 1 week).

A data point holds the information that the official hiscores give and will only be created if the official hiscores are currently working. For all skills, it is required to have enough experience to be in the top two-million accounts to appear in a category. For most bosses, it is required to have a kill count above 50 to appear in a category.

About EHP & EHB

What Is EHP & EHB?
EHP & EHB are units of measurement for efficient hours spent playing the respective Old School RuneScape game mode. Efficient Hours Played (EHP) is a measure of time that indicates having trained skills at the most efficient rate for one hour. Similarly, Efficient Hours Bossed (EHB) is a measure of time that indicates having killed bosses at the most efficient rate for one hour. There are several different types of EHP & EHB for many game modes: Main, Ironman, Ultimate ironman, Level 3, Free-to-play, Free-to-play Ironman, and Fantasy (HLC competition rates).
What Is Bonus Experience?
Some skills offer bonus experience (BXP) due to training several skills in smaller amounts while performing another skill’s main skilling method. Notably, there are two types of bonus experience: BXP-START, and BXP-END.

BXP-START is used when one skilling method gives other skills experience that allows players to skip early levels and training methods. The most common example of BXP-START is Barbarian Rod Fishing, which is sadly no longer efficient in Old School RuneScape but provides a great example without overcomplicating things. With Barbarian Rod Fishing, 1 Agility experience was rewarded for roughly every 11 Fishing experience from level 48 Fishing to 200 million experience in Fishing. This allowed a player to skip nearly 18 million Agility experience before having to do another method to efficiently train the skill.

BXP-END is used when one skilling method gives other skills experience, but the player cannot skip early levels and methods. Common examples of BXP-END are either Firemaking experience from training Woodcutting or Smithing experience from training Mining. Both of these methods use infernal tools, which require you to train at early levels before unlocking the tools needed for the method.
How Is Bonus Experience Calculated?
If the most efficient way to train a skill is determined to be a method that gains experience in multiple skills, such as Mining, EHP will be spread across all skills in the method. The long-term average of training all skills via this method should be 1 EHP per efficient hour played. For example, to obtain 1 EHP you would have to train both 125,000 experience in Mining and 10,000 experience in Smithing.

A popular request is asking how much of only one skill do you need to train to get 1 EHP. To calculate this you will need to do some basic math, we have provided an example below.

Smithing EHP: 415,000 Smithing XP/H

Mining EHP: 125,000 Mining XP/H, 0.08 Smithing BXP Ratio

0.08 * 125,000 = 10,000 Smithing XP per 125,000 Mining XP

10,000 Smithing XP / 415,000 Smithing XP/H = 0.0241 EHP from Smithing per hour of Mining

125,000 Mining XP / (1 - 0.0241 EHP) = 128,086.420 XP/H Mining = 1 "EHP"
How Accurate is EHP/EHB?
The rates for EHP & EHB on our website are reviewed several times throughout the year and adjusted after discussion between our administration staff and the top ~500 players of the appropriate game mode.

We recognize that some rates may be inconsistent compared to other rates by a small margin, but strive to adjust them to match what is agreed upon by our community. Rates are set as the most common realistic method, and therefore may not always equal the highest possible experience per hour in a skill.

Please note that other websites may offer different rates for EHP & EHB due to their difference in perspective within Old School Runescape’s community. Often these rates are outdated or prone to several calculation errors.

General

Can I Update Myself Automatically?
Unfortunately, there is no official auto-updater. If you decide to create your own please be mindful of the number of requests you send to our server. We suggest a maximum of 5 requests per minute to our website to prevent being rate limited.

If you need something to automate adding data points for your clan members, please take a look at the XP Updater plugin on RuneLite!

Keep in mind your account profile will only update if the official hiscores are working.

How Do I Get A Flag On My Profile Page?
Country flags are given via the #request-flag channel in our Discord server.

Please only request flags for your accounts!

Flags are meant to be a meaningful way to filter our leaderboards by country. We ask that you do not request a flag for your profile that you do not have a residence in for this purpose.
How Do I Join The Various Discussion Channels?
To join the various discussion channels you must be top 500 in the category relevant to the discussion in that channel on TempleOSRS, with some exceptions. Discussion channels for the following categories exist EHP, EHB, Ironman, Ultimate ironman, Free-to-play, Level 3, and RuneLite.

The goal for these channels is to have respectful discussions and obtain public feedback before our administration staff applies any changes to the relevant EHP & EHB.

Any roles or permissions in these channels will be taken away if you spam, harass, or troll. If you feel like someone needs the relevant role please let an Administrator know on our Discord server. Serious discussion only.

Records

What Are The Rules For Records?
Please note that the term "5m record" and "6h record" are often used interchangeably[¹].

TempleOSRS staff reserve the right to mark a record as fake for any reason and the record to not be shown on the records page. These leaderboards are to be used as a tool for a competitive environment, where micro efficiency is rewarded. Any submission that attempts to cheese a record by doing methods you would not do outside of a recorded attempt may be marked. Any submission for a record that does not abide by Jagex TOS will also be marked (such as illegal third-party clients or plugins).

Common examples for a record to be marked fake include:
  • A bad data point
  • Abusing antilog for a longer session in methods that do not require them
  • The use of stored experience (Tears, Sq'irk juice, Tickets, etc.)
  • Going against the spirit of creating a competitive leaderboard
  • Not recording a competitive top 3 skilling record[²]
  • VOD evidence of the player using an illegal client or plugins

[¹]: Five-minute (5m) records get their name due to the record page using a time period of five minutes between two data points for the leaderboard. However, it is important to note that due to the game's mechanics you can play for approximately six hours before the game servers attempt to forcefully log your account out, and your official hiscores update. This means that you can carry over six hours of experience into a time period. This means that the five-minute category is a cumulative playtime of six hours and five minutes.

[²]: Record yourself doing any competitive/contested five-minute record attempts, top 3 records will be marked fake moving forward without video evidence. Video links will be publicly posted on the records page next to your record, and these will be used as evidence on particularly contested skill records.
When Do Days/Months/Years Reset?
A day never resets, our website will always be a comparison between your earliest and latest data point within the chosen time interval. A day is always 24 hours, a month is always 31 days, and a year is always 365 days.

You can check when the earliest data point within a time period "falls off" by selecting your time period on the player page, and then looking for the section that states "Xp drop in: ". You can interpret this section as "How much time do I have left to get the biggest number for this time period".
Why Are My Current Records & Achievements Not Showing?
TL;DR: Visit our Discord server and post in #help with your RSN!

If your skill record has been marked in red on your profile page then your record has been marked as a non-competitive fake record. These records are often marked because of a disqualifying material listed in the “Rules” subsection above. If you feel like your record was incorrectly marked, feel free to visit our Discord server and send a message in #help.

If your record has not been marked in red, your profile may be temporarily marked as disqualified on the website. This usually occurs when you have two or more conflicting Old School Runescape data points on your TempleOSRS profile. You can find your profile’s disqualification status within the “Tools” tab on your profile page. If your status states LIMITED in yellow, please visit our Discord server and send a message in #help so a member of our staff can assist you in correcting your profile.

There is also a small chance that the record page you are attempting to view has not been cached yet. Every 15 minutes our website iterates over the next available skill and boss in turn order. You can see how long it has been since the last cache in the lower left-hand corner of the web page. It takes roughly six hours and fifteen minutes to go through all skill categories, and fourteen hours and thirty minutes to go through all boss categories.
How Do You Set a 6h/Five-Minute Record?
Here is a very basic foolproof three step tutorial on getting your 6h (Also known as five-minute record) to count on the site:
  • When your in-game RL timer shows you have been logged in for 5:59:00 update your TempleOSRS profile
  • Play until you get logged out of the game by the server
  • Update your TempleOSRS profile as soon as you can after getting logged out

Warning!
This might still fail if you are doing a record that keeps you in combat for longer than 3-4 minutes at a time, to avoid this messing up your record, manually log out from the game at latest before 6:03:00.

You should understand the updating process more in-depth if you are looking to set a top record and want to squeeze couple more minutes into your record.
More information below or if you want to watch a lecture by Lavine on youtube.
To set a five-minute record an account must be played for 6 hours, and the corresponding account player page on TempleOSRS must be updated before the server forcefully logs the account out. This captures the account data before the start of the account's game session.

The account will then get forcefully logged out of the game session after approximately six hours. The user can then opt to log back into the game to gain more experience before the initial data point's five-minute deadline to gain more experience.

The account must be updated within five minutes of their "blank" starting data point. It is important to note that the same logic can be applied for any time period.

If you are unsure about anything regarding 6h/5min records don't hesitate to ask on our Discord servers #help channel.
How Do I Properly Five-Minute Into A Competition?
Example competition start time: December 31, 2021 7:00 PM (Pt. C)

If the directions are followed correctly, all account progress since December 31, 2021 1:05 PM (Pt. B) will count towards the competition starting on December 31, 2021 7:00 PM (Pt. C).

  1. Find the time six hours before the competition: December 31, 2021 1:00 PM (Pt. A)
  2. Log in shortly after six hours before the competition: December 31, 2021 1:05 PM (Pt. B)
  3. Do not log out or hop worlds. Play as hard as you can!
  4. Create data points after the competition starts: December 31, 2021 7:00 PM (Pt. X)
  5. Use RuneLite or set a timer to know when you are about to be logged out
  6. Logout
  7. Create another data point after logging out: December 31, 2021 7:05 PM (Pt. Y)
  8. Make sure that you have a data point before the end of the competition window (Pt. D)

Competitions & Groups

Questions About Group Icons
Group icons and banners are set by an administrator for your group if your group is either a featured group or a verified clan.

If you are in multiple clans and the featured icon on your account page is not the clan you wish to represent, please visit our Discord server and post in #help!
How Does My Group Become A Featured Group?
Featured groups are a donation perk. For more information please visit our Discord server and read #donation-info.
How Does My Group Become A Verified Clan?
Verified clans are hand-selected by our administrative staff & the site owner. While there is no formal policy in place, the clans that have the verified flag are usually community staples in Old School Runescape. They have been around for quite some time, have well-known members, and have notable accomplishments.

Please do not harass staff members about your group becoming a verified clan. Please have one of your clan leaders visit our Discord server and post in #help to start a verification request.
Why Isn't Everyone Showing In My Competition/Group?
Accounts may not be shown in groups or competitions if they have never been tracked on the website before. Have your group enable RuneLite’s auto-updater if you do not wish to update a massive clan list. See the FAQ titled “Can I Update Myself Automatically?” in the “Basic Questions” section on how to do this properly.
Why Am I Not Being Tracked In A Competition/Group?
There are three reasons why this occurs.

  1. Your account has been temporarily marked as disqualified on the website due to having data points from several Old School RuneScape accounts. See the FAQ titled “Why Are My Current Records & Achievements Not Showing?” in the “Records” section on how to remedy this.

  2. An initial data point for the account was not created at the start of the competition. Only the difference between the first and the last data point counts in a competition. See the FAQ titled “How Do I Properly Five-Minute Into A Competition?” in the “Records” section on how to do this properly.

  3. The OSRS hiscores have not been updated in a while due to Jagex's current server load. To attempt to fix this hop across each server type, and then manually click the logout button.

Collection Log

How Do I Syncronize My Items?
- Open your configuration tab in RuneLite. (Red highlight)
- Navigate to the plugin hub. (Blue highlight)
- Search for "templeosrs" and install the plugin. (Yellow highlight)
- Install the TempleOSRS plugin. (Pink highlight)



- Open your collection log interface and there should be button on the top right corner to sync your items with TempleOSRS server. (Red highlight)


Is There Way To Automatically Syncronize New Items?
Yes it can be enabled from the TempleOSRS plugin settings highlighted red in the picture below. It will automatically sync all new collections you receive in the background, no opening any menus or pressing buttons.

Note that automatically synced items do not contain quantity data, so if you wish to have the accurate amount displayed for each new item in your log, you still need to manually sync once in a while.

For the automatic sync to work you must have the "Collection Log - New addition notification" setting enabled in-game. (Green highlight)


Why Isn't My Collection Log Syncronization Button Not showing up?
If you aren't seeing the syncronization button in your collection log interface and there are NO other buttons in its place, make sure it isn't turned off from the plugin settings.

If there ARE other buttons in its place, you can turn the TempleOSRS plugin on and off for it to appear or you can disable the other plugins momentarily. I'm working on a fix for the issue of the buttons overriding each other.
Why Isn't My Plugin Syncronization Working?
Nearly all collection log sync issues are caused by not having up-to-date data on the XP/KC tracking side of the site. To check if this is the case for you, go to your profiles stats tab and check what it says for your total collection log count. If it is out of date, navigate to your profiles overview page and press the big green button. To avoid this happening again in the future, use the default XP Updater plugin on RuneLite or press the button every 20-50 new collections.

To sync your items on the site you need to have a profile. Just search your name on the navigation bar and press update if there is no profile. It's good idea to update it regardless to get your hiscores collection log count to be accurate.

If you are syncing your collection log for the first time make sure there are no existing profiles under your RSN on TempleOSRS. If there are, you will need to submit name change for them before syncing your collection log. If you can't find the new name or the name change is declined post on our Discord servers #help channel.

Syncronization can also fail if you have gained lot of items after your last update on TempleOSRS. The total item count is compared to your hiscores collection count and if you haven't updated yourself on the site for a while it might deny the sync request due to not matching the total collection count on our server.

Update your TempleOSRS profile and attempt to sync again after restarting your client, if it still fails please post on our Discord servers #help channel and I will investigate it.

Automatic sync:


If any of your new collections fails to sync automatically and you have read the "Is There Way To Automatically Syncronize New Items?" FAQ instructions above, please post on our Discord servers #help channel and tag @44mikael and specify which item failed to sync and I will look into it.
