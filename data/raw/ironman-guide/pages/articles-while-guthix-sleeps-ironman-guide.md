# While Guthix Sleeps Ironman Guide (OSRS 2026): Requirements, Quest Chain, Prep Checklist & Rewards

While Guthix Sleeps for OSRS ironmen: every requirement and prerequisite quest, fastest routes to 72 Thieving and 65 Herblore, a prep checklist and HCIM risks.

27 min read·Updated October 22, 2026·Difficulty: ****

**While Guthix Sleeps** (WGS) is a Grandmaster quest and, since **20 October 2026**, the only entry requirement for **The Fractured Archive**, the fourth raid. It is also one of the longest unlocks in the game: six unboostable skill levels between 62 and 72, 180 quest points, Warriors' Guild entry, and 41 prerequisite quests plus a miniquest.

This page is the ironman prep sheet, not a walkthrough. The OSRS Wiki already has the [full walkthrough](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/While_Guthix_Sleeps) and the [quick guide](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/While_Guthix_Sleeps/Quick_guide); keep one open when you start. Everything below is about getting to that point without a Grand Exchange: what each requirement costs you, what order to do the chain in, what to bank before you talk to Ivy Sophista in Taverley, and which fights can end a hardcore. Every number is from the wiki as of October 2026.

The three walls

1. **65 Herblore.** The wiki's ironman Herblore guide calls it arguably the hardest skill to train on an ironman, and expects around 67 to 69 by the time all hard diaries are done if you make every potion along the way. For most irons, WGS is ready when the hard diaries are.
2. **72 Thieving and 66 Agility.** Long, but the methods pay you back: Thieving funds Miscellania and Agility gets you graceful.
3. **The levels hidden in the chain.** 61 Crafting, 60 Mining and 55 Woodcutting for Lunar Diplomacy, 60 Strength and 56 Slayer for The Path of Glouphrie, 55 Smithing for Defender of Varrock and 85 combat for Dream Mentor. None of them are boostable.

### Warriors' Guild entry

Ghommal only lets you in with a combined **Attack and Strength of 130**, or 99 in either, and the wiki says temporary boosts do not count. You need to get inside during the quest to recruit Ghommal, Harrallak Menarous and Sloane.

The chain does most of the work. The Path of Glouphrie forces **60 Strength** (unboostable), so **70 Attack** finishes the sum. The quests on the way pay a lot of melee XP: The Grand Tree 18,400 Attack, Waterfall Quest 13,750 Attack and 13,750 Strength, Fight Arena 12,175 Attack, Tree Gnome Village 11,450 Attack, Death Plateau 3,000 Attack, and The Path of Glouphrie itself 30,000 Strength.

## The prerequisite quest tree

WGS lists ten direct prerequisites: Defender of Varrock, The Path of Glouphrie, Fight Arena, Dream Mentor, The Hand in the Sand, Wanted!, Temple of the Eye, Tears of Guthix, Nature Spirit and A Tail of Two Cats. Their chains pull in 31 more quests and the Enter the Abyss miniquest, for 41 quests and 77 quest points in total. These are the highest skill levels anywhere in that tree:

All of those are unboostable. One more is easy to miss: The Path of Glouphrie needs a **mith grapple** and a crossbow, and an ironman has to make the grapple (**59 Fletching** and **59 Smithing**, both boostable) or get it as a drop in the Ancient Cavern, which opens after the first part of Barbarian Firemaking at 35 Firemaking.

### 67 Magic

Our [Magic guide](https://ironman.guide/articles/osrs-ironman-magic-guide) has the full route. The short version: five quests to 34, then the Arceuus Library (15 XP per Magic level per book, around 120-150 books an hour, so 108,000-135,000 XP an hour at 60) and the Mage Training Arena from 33. The chain pays 12,000 from The Eyes of Glouphrie and 2,150 from The Grand Tree on the way up, then 5,000 from Lunar Diplomacy, 10,000 from Dream Mentor plus its 15,000 XP dreamy lamp, and 5,000 from The Path of Glouphrie once you are past Lunar Diplomacy's 65.

### 49 Firemaking

The wiki route is to cut and burn oak logs to 39 and teak logs from there; teaks are south-west of Castle Wars or south of Civitas illa Fortis. Wintertodt needs 50, so it comes straight after.

The wiki's recommended extras: 95 combat, a Taverley teleport, fairy ring access (AKS for Feldip Hills, ALS for McGrubor's Wood), Falador teleports or a ring of returning set to Falador, antipoison, and a combat bracelet, games necklace or Strength cape for the Warriors' Guild. Bring a **coal bag** and **log basket** too: a side room in Movario's base holds 100 death runes, 100 fire runes, 100 coal and 100 magic logs, all unnoted. Hand Movario's notes to Thaerisk before you go back for it: the quickest way out with the loot is to fail the weight puzzle on purpose, which jails you and confiscates the notes even if they are in your bank.

You do **not** need to bring: the mort myre fungus, a log (a bronze axe is available next to the hunting expert if you carry no axe), pink dye, the snapdragon seed (Thaerisk gives one free), papyrus and charcoal, the restore potion, the single runes for the battered door (a chest in the base has them), or any herbs. The druid spirits in the Ancient Guthixian Temple drop exactly the ingredients your eight statues need, and the spade, hammer and chisel are on a skeleton at the bottom of the chasm.

### Runes for the Surok Magis fight

Surok is the boss that takes the most planning on an ironman, because he can only be damaged by combat spells and his special attacks each need a specific counter spell. Powered staves do nothing to him; Iban Blast works. Per-cast costs from the wiki:

| Spell | Level | Runes per cast | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wind / Water / Earth / Fire Blast | 41 / 47 / 53 / 59 | 1 death plus air, water, earth and fire | Damage, and countering his elemental surges with the opposite element |
| Wind / Water / Earth / Fire Wave | 62 / 65 / 70 / 75 | 1 blood plus elementals | The same, once you can cast all four |
| Bind / Snare / Entangle | 20 / 50 / 79 | 2 / 3 / 4 nature plus earth and water | The Agile Warrior |
| Weaken / Enfeeble | 11 / 73 | 1 body / 1 soul plus earth and water | The Strong Warrior |
| Telekinetic Grab | 33 | 1 law, 1 air | Grabbing the lit explosive |
| Low / High Level Alchemy | 21 / 55 | 1 nature plus 3 / 5 fire | Alching the explosive straight after |

The wiki says to bring the highest spell tier you can cast in **all four** elements. With 67 to 74 Magic that is the Blast tier on death runes, because Fire Wave needs 75. If you use RuneLite's "No Bad Alchs" plugin, turn it off for this fight.

## Dangerous fights for hardcore ironmen

Nothing in While Guthix Sleeps is a safe death

The wiki's Hardcore Ironman safe-death list includes the Dream World in Lunar Diplomacy and the first three rounds against Koschei in The Fremennik Trials. Nothing else in the chain is on it, and neither is any part of While Guthix Sleeps. Every fight below can end a hardcore.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| The Inadequacy, The Everlasting, The Untouchable, The Illusive | Dream Mentor | 343 / 223 / 274 / 108 | Not safe; no prayer allowed; no teleports, ring of life included; leave through the lectern |

A few things that change how risky those fights are:

- **Teleports are blocked** inside Movario's base from the stairs down to the old battered door until you solve the weight puzzle. Using the wrong rune on the door, or opening a trapped chest without searching it first, deals 18 to 20+ damage.
- **The Balance Elemental can be hidden from.** It routefinds around melee safespots, but the wiki describes ducking behind the Stone of Jas or a pillar to reset, and the quick guide says lower levels and hardcores can use a pillar for the entire fight. Bring an emergency teleport that is not runes, because the elemental can drain your Magic, plus super restores and food; Menaphite remedy (88 Herblore) helps if you have one. Travelling into the chasm removes any boosts, so drink potions after you arrive. If you die, your gravestone is in the Chasm of Tears with Movario and Darve.
- **You can sit out the tormented demons.** Hide behind the south-western column and the Guardians of Armadyl kill them, which takes upwards of 10 minutes. Don't let the game log you out for idling, because that resets the fight. Teleporting out to restock resets it to the demons rather than the elemental.

See the [HCIM guide](https://ironman.guide/hcim) for the general rules.

## Where it fits in your ironman progression

WGS is late-game on an ironman. The wiki's ironman optimal quest guide places it near the very end, after Monkey Madness II, Dragon Slayer II and Secrets of the North and just before Song of the Elves, and it tells you to train Farming to 65 and Thieving to 72 right before it. On our [full walkthrough](https://ironman.guide/guide) it sits in [Goals after Barrows gloves](https://ironman.guide/guide/after-barrows-gloves), after you have started the Tombs of Amascut.

What WGS opens up comes in two stages:

1. **Straight away:** Tormented Demons in the Ancient Guthixian Temple, for burning claws and the synapse weapons. They also drop the Guthixian temple teleport scrolls that bring you back.
2. **Later:** The Fractured Archive. The quest gets you in, but the wiki suggests 90+ Hitpoints, Attack, Strength, Defence and Ranged with 96+ Magic for the raid, and the miniquest recommends 110 combat. Keep your sapphire lantern: the light creatures in the Chasm of Tears are how you get down to the archive until you have teleport scrolls or the Archivist's folio.

Quest XP is fixed, so there is nothing to gain by waiting once you have the levels: the Tormented Demon drops start paying the day you finish.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the requirements for While Guthix Sleeps in OSRS?

180 quest points, 72 Thieving, 67 Magic, 66 Agility, 65 Farming, 65 Herblore and 62 Hunter (none of them boostable), 49 Firemaking to light a sapphire lantern, and entry to the Warriors' Guild (Attack plus Strength of at least 130, or 99 in either). You also need ten quests and everything behind them: Defender of Varrock, The Path of Glouphrie, Fight Arena, Dream Mentor, The Hand in the Sand, Wanted!, Temple of the Eye, Tears of Guthix, Nature Spirit and A Tail of Two Cats. The OSRS Wiki recommends 95 combat.

### Can you boost for While Guthix Sleeps?

No. The wiki marks the Thieving, Magic, Agility, Farming, Herblore and Hunter levels and the Warriors' Guild entry as unboostable, and the guild itself refuses temporary boosts. The 49 Firemaking is only for lighting the sapphire lantern, but Lunar Diplomacy already needs 49 Firemaking unboosted. The levels hidden in the prerequisite chain are unboostable too: 61 Crafting, 60 Mining and 55 Woodcutting for Lunar Diplomacy, 60 Strength and 56 Slayer for The Path of Glouphrie, and 55 Smithing for Defender of Varrock.

### What is the hardest While Guthix Sleeps requirement for an ironman?

65 Herblore, for most accounts. The wiki's ironman Herblore guide calls Herblore arguably the hardest skill to train on an ironman, because every herb and secondary has to be gathered, and expects around 67 to 69 Herblore by the time all hard diaries are done if you make every potion along the way. Quests take you to 26, Eadgar's Ruse in the chain adds 11,000 XP, and Mastering Mixology from 60 gives more XP per herb. 72 Thieving and 66 Agility take longer in hours but are simple to train.

### Is While Guthix Sleeps safe for hardcore ironmen?

No. None of its fights are on the wiki's list of safe deaths for Hardcore Ironmen, so dying to the assassins, Surok Magis, the Balance Elemental or the tormented demons costs you your status. The chain adds more: the wiki says Dream Mentor's dream world is not safe and blocks every teleport including the ring of life, and dying to Sir Leye in Recruitment Drive also ends a hardcore. Only Lunar Diplomacy's Dream World and the first three rounds against Koschei in The Fremennik Trials are listed as safe.

### What does While Guthix Sleeps unlock?

5 quest points and 280,000 XP (80,000 Thieving, 75,000 Farming, 75,000 Herblore and 50,000 Hunter), the Ancient Guthixian Temple and its Tormented Demons, Duradel's notes from Kuradal (one-time Slayer XP and the ability to make the Emberlight, scorching bow and purging staff from tormented synapses), the Black Knight Catacombs, elite black armour, and entry to The Fractured Archive raid.

### How do you unlock The Fractured Archive in OSRS?

Complete While Guthix Sleeps. It is the only hard requirement. The raid sits at the bottom of the Chasm of Tears, which you reach with a games necklace to Tears of Guthix and a lit sapphire lantern, and The Fractured Archive miniquest starts the first time you try to enter the lobby. The miniquest recommends 110 combat, and deaths inside the raid are not safe for hardcore ironmen.

### What quests do you need for While Guthix Sleeps?

Ten direct prerequisites and their chains: 41 quests plus the Enter the Abyss miniquest, worth 77 quest points between them. The ones that gate the most skills are Lunar Diplomacy (61 Crafting, 60 Mining, 55 Woodcutting, 65 Magic), Dream Mentor (85 combat), Defender of Varrock (55 Smithing, 52 Hunter) and The Path of Glouphrie (60 Strength, 56 Slayer, 56 Thieving, 47 Ranged, 45 Agility).
