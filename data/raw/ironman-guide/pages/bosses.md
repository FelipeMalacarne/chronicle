# OSRS Ironman Boss Order & Bossing Ladder 2026

Recommended boss progression for OSRS Ironman accounts, updated for 2026. Includes Moons of Peril, Royal Titans, Sailing content, and all major bosses.

37 bosses New (2025+) Milestone Slayer boss

## Early Game

Pre-Bowfa progression. Barrows, Moons of Peril, Royal Titans, and Fight Caves.

1

![Barrows](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Ahrim%27s_robetop.png)

### Barrows

solo

Style: Magic (Iban's Blast / Twinflame Staff)

Requires: Priest in Peril, 50+ Magic recommended

Priority drops:

Ahrim's Robetop/Robeskirt Important Karil's Crossbow Nice to have Runes (blood, death, chaos) Important

Excellent early source of runes and magic armour. Twinflame Staff makes this very efficient. Do 50-100 chests for rune supply and Ahrim's pieces.

2

![Dagannoth Rex](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Berserker_ring.png)

### Dagannoth Rex

solo

Style: Magic

Requires: No quest required, 50+ Magic (Iban's Blast) or a powered staff

Priority drops:  
Berserker Ring Priority Warrior Ring Nice to have

Camp Rex only — he has very low Magic defence, so safespotting with Iban's Blast or a powered staff melts him. Imbue the Berserker Ring at Soul Wars or NMZ for +8 strength. Can be done during a Dagannoth Slayer task.

3

![Eclipse Moon](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Eclipse_atlatl.png)

### Eclipse Moon

2025+

solo/duo

Style: Melee (stab)

Requires: Perilous Moons quest, 75 Ranged, 50 Defence

Priority drops:

Eclipse Atlatl Priority Eclipse Moon Helm Important Eclipse Moon Chestplate Important Eclipse Moon Tassets Important

The most-farmed moon for early ironmen. Eclipse Atlatl (1/224) is a two-handed ranged weapon whose max hit scales with your MELEE strength bonus — with full Eclipse armour it gains a burn set effect and carries early PvM and Slayer. Weak to stab; a slow, heavy hitter helps on the clone phase.

4

![Blue Moon](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Blue_moon_helm.png)

### Blue Moon

2025+

solo/duo

Style: Melee

Requires: Perilous Moons quest, 75 Magic, 50 Defence

Priority drops:

Blue Moon Helm Important Blue Moon Chestplate Priority Blue Moon Tassets Important

Priority from Moons of Peril. Blue Moon magic armour provides +30 magic attack (body) needed for Royal Titans. Farm full set before moving on.

5

![Blood Moon](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Blood_moon_helm.png)

### Blood Moon

2025+

solo/duo

Style: Melee (slash)

Requires: Perilous Moons quest, 75 Strength, 50 Defence

Priority drops:

Blood Moon Helm Important Blood Moon Chestplate Important Blood Moon Tassets Important Dual Macuahuitl (from Lunar Chest) Priority

The Moons have +500 ranged and magic defence, so melee is the only viable style — Blood Moon is weak to slash. Bring your best slash weapon. Blood Moon melee set has the Bloodrager set effect with Dual Macuahuitl and is strong for Slayer.

6

![Royal Titans](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Twinflame_staff.png)

### Royal Titans

2025+Milestone

solo/duo

Style: All three styles (melee/ranged/magic switches)

Requires: No quest required, ~90 combat recommended

Priority drops:

Deadeye prayer scroll Priority Mystic Vigour prayer scroll Priority Fire Element Staff Crown Priority Ice Element Staff Crown Priority Giantsoul Amulet Nice to have

Do BEFORE Corrupted Gauntlet. Deadeye (+18% ranged) and Mystic Vigour (+18% magic) are permanent prayer upgrades that help at CG. Crown drops (1/75 each) combine into Twinflame Staff. Wear Blue Moon armour for magic phases. Can be done on a Fire Giant Slayer task.

7

![Amoxliatl](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Glacial_temotli.png)

### Amoxliatl

2025+

solo

Style: Melee

Requires: The Heart of Darkness, 60+ melee stats, 37 Prayer

Priority drops:

Glacial Temotli Important Pendant of Ates (Varlamore teleports) Important Frozen Tears (pendant charges) Nice to have

Entry-level boss under the Twilight Temple — attacks with magic - Protect from Magic blocks the initial hit, but the ice-spike follow-up deals typeless damage. Glacial Temotli (1/100) is a two-handed crush weapon that rolls two independent hits. Pendant of Ates (1/25 inert) charges with frozen tears into a six-location Varlamore teleport — a great utility unlock.

8

![TzTok-Jad (Fight Caves)](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Fire_cape.png)

### TzTok-Jad (Fight Caves)

Milestone

solo

Style: Ranged

Requires: 75+ Ranged recommended, prayer switching ability

Priority drops:  
Fire Cape Priority

Major ironman milestone. Practice prayer flicking on Jad. With Deadeye prayer and decent ranged gear this is very achievable.

## Mid Game

Corrupted Gauntlet through ToA. Post-Bowfa bosses, GWD, and Slayer bosses.

9

![Corrupted Gauntlet](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Bow_of_faerdhinen_(c).png)

### Corrupted Gauntlet

Milestone

solo

Style: All styles (Gauntlet provides gear)

Requires: Song of the Elves, high combat stats

Priority drops:

Enhanced Weapon Seed (×2 for Bowfa + Blade) Priority Armour Seeds (×6 for full Crystal) Priority

THE defining mid-game grind. Bowfa + Crystal armour is the strongest ranged setup until Masori. Deadeye prayer from Royal Titans helps significantly here. Expect 100-400+ KC for seeds.

10

![Zulrah](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Toxic_blowpipe.png)

### Zulrah

solo

Style: Ranged + Magic (Bowfa + Trident/Twinflame)

Requires: Regicide quest, 75+ Ranged and Magic

Priority drops:

Toxic Blowpipe (Tanzanite Fang) Priority Magic Fang (Trident of the Swamp upgrade) Priority Serpentine Helm Important

Farm for blowpipe and magic fang. Bowfa makes Zulrah very consistent. Blowpipe is still great for Slayer and specific bosses. Scales + darts needed for charges.

11

![Vorkath](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Ava%27s_assembler.png)

### Vorkath

solo

Style: Ranged (Bowfa) or Melee (Fang)

Requires: Dragon Slayer II

Priority drops:

Vorkath's Head (Ava's Assembler) Priority Dragonbone Necklace Nice to have GP/supplies (consistent money) Important

Guaranteed head at 50 KC. Excellent consistent money maker. Bowfa or Fang both work well. One of the best GP/hr bosses for ironmen.

12

![The Hueycoatl](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Dragon_hunter_wand.png)

### The Hueycoatl

2025+

small team

Style: Magic (earth spells) or Ranged (dragonbane)

Requires: Children of the Sun, 85+ combat and 43 Prayer recommended

Priority drops:

Dragon Hunter Wand Priority Tome of Earth Important Huasca Seeds Important Hueycoatl Hide (ranged armour) Nice to have

Varlamore's group dragon — up to 20 players, comfortable in small teams. 60% weak to earth spells, and its draconic attribute means dragonbane weapons hit hard. Dragon Hunter Wand (1/105) is the dragonbane powered wand for Vorkath, Hydra, and Olm. Huasca Seeds feed Prayer regeneration potions.

13

![Demonic Gorillas](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Amulet_of_torture.png)

### Demonic Gorillas

solo

Style: Melee + Ranged switches

Requires: Monkey Madness II

Priority drops:

Zenyte Shard ×4 (Torture, Anguish, Tormented, Suffering) Priority Ballista pieces Nice to have

Need 4 zenyte shards total for all 4 zenyte jewelry pieces. Requires 89-98 Crafting to cut and 93 Magic to enchant. Bowfa + melee switch works well. Expect 300-600 KC per shard.

14

![Phantom Muspah](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Ancient_sceptre.png)

### Phantom Muspah

2025+

solo

Style: Ranged (Bowfa) + Magic

Requires: Secrets of the North quest

Priority drops:

Ancient Icon (Ancient Sceptre upgrade) Important Saturated Heart Nice to have

Bowfa is very effective here. Ancient Sceptre is useful for Ancient Magicks content. Good rune and resource drops.

15

![Kraken](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Trident_of_the_swamp.png)

### Kraken

Slayer

solo

Style: Magic (Trident / Twinflame)

Requires: 87 Slayer

Priority drops:

Kraken Tentacle (Abyssal Tentacle) Important Trident of the Seas (full) Important

Easy AFK Slayer boss. Get the tentacle for Abyssal Tentacle upgrade. Trident drops here if you don't have one from cave krakens.

16

![General Graardor (Bandos)](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Bandos_chestplate.png)

### General Graardor (Bandos)

solo/duo

Style: Melee (Fang) or Ranged (Bowfa)

Requires: 40 KC in Bandos area, 70 Strength

Priority drops:

Bandos Chestplate Priority Bandos Tassets Priority Bandos Godsword Important

BCP + Tassets are core melee armour. Fang or Bowfa both work. Can solo with good stats. BGS spec is useful for bossing until DWH.

17

![Kree'arra (Armadyl)](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Masori_body_(f).png)

### Kree'arra (Armadyl)

solo/duo

Style: Ranged (Bowfa)

Requires: 40 KC in Armadyl area, 70 Ranged

Priority drops:

Armadyl Chestplate (for Masori (f)) Priority Armadyl Chainskirt (for Masori (f)) Priority Armadyl Helmet (for Masori Mask (f)) Important

Armadyl pieces are needed to fortify Masori armour from ToA. Bowfa with Crystal armour makes this very doable solo. Priority after getting Masori unfortified pieces.

18

![Tombs of Amascut](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Osmumten%27s_fang.png)

### Tombs of Amascut

2025+Milestone

solo/duo

Style: All styles

Requires: Beneath Cursed Sands, high combat stats

Priority drops:

Osmumten's Fang Priority Lightbearer Priority Masori Armour (body, chaps, mask) Important Tumeken's Shadow Important Elidinis' Ward Important

Most ironman-friendly raid with scalable difficulty. Start at entry mode, push to 150+ for uniques. Fang and Lightbearer are the priority drops. Soloable once experienced.

## Late Game

DT2 bosses, raids, high Slayer bosses, Yama, and Doom of Mokhaiotl.

### Vardorvis

2025+

solo

Style: Melee (Fang or Rapier)

Requires: Desert Treasure II

Priority drops:

Ultor Vestige (Ultor Ring) Priority Soulreaper Axe part (1 of 4) Priority Chromium Ingot Important

Easiest DT2 boss. Do this first for Ultor Ring (BIS melee ring, +12 str). The Soulreaper Axe needs one part from EACH of the four DT2 bosses. Fang is very effective here.

### Maggot King

2025+

solo

Style: Ranged or Magic, then Melee

Requires: The Blood Moon Rises quest, near-endgame gear

Priority drops:

Elder Venator Fang (Necklace of Rupture) Priority Crimson Kisten Important

2026 solo instanced boss in Vampyrium, pitched between Phosani's Nightmare and the Inferno. Melee is blocked until ~980 HP remain, so open with ranged or magic (80% fire weakness) and finish in melee. Elder Venator Fang (3/1,000) etches at 84 Crafting onto a Necklace of Anguish to make the Necklace of Rupture. Crimson Kisten (2/1,000) is a strong two-handed crush weapon.

## Endgame

The Inferno. The ultimate PvM achievement.

36

![TzKal-Zuk (Inferno)](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Infernal_cape.png)

### TzKal-Zuk (Inferno)

Milestone

solo

Style: Ranged + Magic

Requires: Fire Cape, near-BIS gear, extensive practice

Priority drops:  
Infernal Cape (BIS melee cape) Priority

The hardest PvM challenge in OSRS. BIS melee cape. Requires mastery of prayer flicking, wave management, and the Zuk fight. Tbow or Bowfa + ACB setup.

37

![The Fractured Archive](https://ironman.guide/images/gear/Obligator.png)

### The Fractured Archive

2025+

small team

Style: All styles (crush-heavy)

Requires: While Guthix Sleeps, 90+ combat stats and 96 Magic suggested

Priority drops:

Horn of Bran (Zeal prayer, Piety upgrade) Priority Ascension Crossbows (raid-tier blowpipe upgrade) Priority Elemental Fragments (+2 spell max hit) Important Zorya's Tome Important Obligator (crush megarare) Important Rondache Nice to have

Raids 4, released 20 October 2026. Scales 2-8 players (Jagex recommends 3-5; solo scales as two). Five bosses per run with three pick-one pairs that award equal contribution. Deaths are not safe for HCIM or HCGIM, and a team wipe leaves a gravestone outside (fees -75% until 5 clears). Most bosses are very weak to crush. Unique rates unpublished as of 21 October 2026.

Notes on boss order

- This order is a recommendation, not a strict requirement. Adjust based on your account and goals.
- Slayer bosses (purple) are done passively during Slayer tasks when you reach the required level.
- DT2 bosses can be done in any order, but Vardorvis first is recommended for the Ultor Ring and Soulreaper Axe.
- Raids (CoX, ToB, ToA) order depends on your team and what drops you need most.
- Rigour and Augury from CoX replace Deadeye and Mystic Vigour from Royal Titans.
