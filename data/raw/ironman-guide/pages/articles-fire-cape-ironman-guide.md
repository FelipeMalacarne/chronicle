# OSRS Fire Cape Guide for Ironmen: Fight Caves Gear, Waves, Jad and HCIM Rules (2026)

How ironmen get the OSRS fire cape: wiki stats, rune crossbow to blowpipe setups, a self-made inventory, every Fight Caves wave, Jad prayers and HCIM rules.

23 min read·Updated October 22, 2026·Difficulty: ***

The fire cape is the reward for clearing the TzHaar Fight Cave: 62 waves of TzHaar, then TzTok-Jad on wave 63, solo and with only the supplies you carried in. The OSRS Wiki's Ironman Guide lists it among the early-game milestones, next to the rune crossbow, Barrows gloves, the fighter torso and the dragon defender. On an iron the hard part is usually supplies: the wiki's early-iron inventory is 17 prayer potions, because Protect from Missiles and Protect from Magic are what get you to Jad.

Everything below comes from the wiki's Fight Cave, strategy, TzTok-Jad, fire cape, Tokkul and safe-death pages, checked on 22 October 2026.

Fire cape at a glance

- **Requirements:** none. The wiki's early-game iron setup suggests 61+ Ranged, 70+ Hitpoints and 43+ Prayer.
- **Length:** 63 waves. With mid-level iron gear the wiki expects well over 60 minutes, usually closer to 90+.
- **Death:** safe for normal and hardcore ironmen; you keep everything except the supplies you used. Not safe for Hardcore Group Ironmen.
- **Reward:** the fire cape, 8,032 Tokkul (16,064 with the elite Karamja Diary) and a 1/200 TzRek-Jad pet roll.
- **Getting there:** the "TzHaar Fight Pit" Minigame Teleport, or fairy ring BLP and run north.

## What the Fire Cape Gives You

The fire cape is a melee cape. The wiki calls it the second best in the game after the infernal cape, and you need a fire cape to enter the Inferno, so the two come in that order.

| Cape | Attack | Defence (all styles) | Strength | Prayer | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infernal cape | +4 stab, slash and crush, +1 magic and ranged | +12 | +8 | +2 | The Inferno |
| **Fire cape** | **+1 all styles** | **+11** | **+4** | **+2** | **TzHaar Fight Cave** |
| Mythical cape | +6 crush | +8 | +1 | +1 | Dragon Slayer II |
| Obsidian cape | None | +9 | None | None | 90,000 Tokkul in Mor Ul Rek |
| Cape of Legends | None | +7 | None | None | Legends' Guild shop |

The +4 Strength is why it matters. Nothing reachable early comes close to it for melee, and you keep it for the rest of the account unless you go for the infernal cape. It is untradeable, so if you lose one the only replacement is another clear. An unprotected PvP death breaks it rather than deleting it, and Perdu repairs it for 150,000 coins.

What showing the cape unlocks matters just as much on an iron. You show it to a TzHaar-Ket guard once and the inner city of Mor Ul Rek is open for good:

- **TzHaar-Hur-Rin's Ore and Gem Store,** the same stock as the outer gem shop but much larger: 16 uncut sapphires, 12 emeralds, 8 rubies and 6 diamonds against one sapphire and one emerald outside.
- **Infernal eels** at 80 Fishing with an oily fishing rod, bait and ice gloves. Cracking them with a hammer gives Tokkul, onyx bolt tips or lava scale shards, which go into extended antifires at 84 Herblore.
- **TzHaar-Hur pickpocketing** at 90 Thieving, with ice gloves suggested.
- **Obsidian armour** from TzHaar-Hur-Zal's Equipment Store, plus a bank next to a sulphur vent you can cook on.
- **The Inferno** (you give up one fire cape to enter) and the first two of TzHaar-Ket-Rak's Challenges.

The Karamja Diary uses the cave twice. The hard tier has you kill a Ket-Zek, which first appears on wave 31. The elite tier needs you to equip a fire cape or infernal cape in Mor Ul Rek, and its reward is double Tokkul and one free resurrection a day inside the cave.

## Gear: Three Iron Setups

The wiki recommends Ranged for most of the run, because it lets you safespot monsters and hit them from a distance. Melee is faster at the top end, but the strategy page calls it a poor option for a first fire cape. Magic has been a real option since 25 June 2025, when every TzHaar got a 40% weakness to water spells, but it needs 2,000 to 2,500 casts of runes, which is a lot of shopping on an iron. See the [Magic guide](https://ironman.guide/articles/osrs-ironman-magic-guide) for rune sources if you want to try it.

The wiki's early-game iron setup assumes "tier-50 and tier-60 gear and maybe a barrows piece or two". The three setups below go from that up to a blowpipe. Every item is on one of the wiki's recommended lists, so swap in whatever you own from the same list.

## The Waves and Monsters

The cave runs 63 waves. Each new monster type first appears on wave 1, 3, 7, 15, 31 and finally 63. The rule is simple: when a wave has two of the same monster, the next wave replaces them with one monster of the next level up, so two 22s become a 45, two 90s become a 180, and so on. If every monster in a wave is different, another Tz-Kih joins. Wave 62 is two Ket-Zeks, and wave 63 is Jad.

| Level | Monster | HP | First wave | Attacks and max hit | What to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 22 | Tz-Kih | 10 | 1 | Melee, 4 | Drains Prayer in melee range by the damage dealt plus 1. Kill it first. |
| 45 | Tz-Kek | 20 | 3 | Melee, 7 | Deals 1 recoil damage to melee attackers. Splits into two level 22 Tz-Keks when it dies. Lowest priority. |
| 90 | Tok-Xil | 40 | 7 | Ranged 14, melee 13 | Accurate long-range attacker. Pray Ranged against it early on. Its melee is weaker if you have to walk up to it. |
| 180 | Yt-MejKot | 80 | 15 | Melee, 25 | Heals itself and nearby monsters below half health. Easily trapped behind rocks. |
| 360 | Ket-Zek | 160 | 31 | Magic 52, melee 55 | Pray Magic whenever one is alive and stay out of its melee range. |
| 702 | TzTok-Jad | 250 | 63 | Ranged 97, melee 97, magic 95 | See the Jad section. |
| 108 | Yt-HurKot | 60 | 63 | Melee, 14 | Four spawn when Jad drops below 150 Hitpoints and heal him. |

Across a full run you kill 48 Tz-Kih, 40 level 45 Tz-Keks (and the 80 level 22s they split into), 36 Tok-Xil, 34 Yt-MejKot, 33 Ket-Zeks, Jad and his four healers.

**Kill order**, per the strategy page: Tz-Kih, then Tok-Xil, then Ket-Zek, then Yt-MejKot, then Tz-Kek. The exception is a monster trapped behind a rock: stay where you are and kill what you can reach first. When two of the same monster spawn, one is off-colour, and that one marks where the next tier spawns.

### Safespots and rotations

The Fight Cave map has three rocks worth knowing:

- **Italy Rock,** on the eastern side. Stand directly against its western or southern wall and level 45 Tz-Keks and level 180 Yt-MejKots get stuck on the corners.
- **Long Rock** (stand on its south side) and **Dragon Rock** (west side). Both trap every spawn except the one that appears right next to them.
- **The north-eastern corner** from about wave 38. The wiki says three of the five spawn points get stuck behind Italy Rock and the rock to its west.

There are 15 rotations of spawn points, chosen by the exact time you enter. The rotation changes every minute, except rotation 4, which lasts two. You can identify yours from where the first two waves spawn (four waves for two of them). For early-game irons the strategy page singles out rotations 5 and 6 as especially safespot-friendly.

Jad always spawns where the wave 3 level 45 Tz-Kek spawned, and the off-colour Ket-Zek on wave 62 spawns there too. The best spawn is south of Italy Rock. If you don't get it, leave and restart. Since the 12 August 2026 Summer Sweep Up there is no re-entry cooldown, so the reset only costs you the first three waves.

### Prayer through the waves

- **Tok-Xil waves (7-30):** use Protect from Missiles. Wave 30 is the last one with no Ket-Zek.
- **Ket-Zek waves (31-62):** pray Magic whenever a Ket-Zek is alive, so the Tok-Xils have to die fast or be fought in melee range.
- **Flick your offensive prayer** (Eagle Eye, Hawk Eye or Rigour) on the Ket-Zeks to save Prayer.
- **Turn off Auto Retaliate.** Tok-Xils and Ket-Zeks can drag you out of a safespot into Yt-MejKot range.
- **Get a free hit:** when a safespotted monster is behind another one, kill the free one, then hit the trapped one straight away. The dying monster's animation gives you a free attack.

## TzTok-Jad

Jad has 250 Hitpoints, attacks every 8 ticks from up to 15 tiles away, and hits up to 97. Stay out of melee range and he only uses two attacks:

- **Magic:** he rears up and stands up straight, then breathes a fireball. You will hear a loud inhale. Protect from Magic has to go on while he is rearing up; once the fire comes out it is too late.
- **Ranged:** he slams his front legs into the ground, cracks appear, and a boulder lands on you. The sound plays after the hit, so the wiki suggests having Protect from Missiles on when the wave starts.

If you stand next to him he also bites (Protect from Melee, no warning, and he can attack every 4 ticks in melee). Fight from range. The wiki's advice when you take damage is to eat once per Jad attack and pray first. Don't panic-eat.

### Healers

When Jad drops below 150 Hitpoints, four Yt-HurKots spawn and walk to him to heal him.

- **Tag all four without killing them.** Leaving even one on Jad can heal him back up while you kill the others. They still heal Jad while hitting you if they are close enough to him.
- **If they heal him to full, don't kill them.** Dead healers respawn the next time he drops below 150. If you distract them before he reaches full health, they won't come back once killed.
- **Switch to long range** before tagging healers so you don't step into Jad's melee range.
- **Black chinchompas** make the healers easier, but you have to target each healer, because Jad is too big for the splash to hit them all. They need 65 Ranged, and catching them takes 73 Hunter in the Wilderness, which is not on the safe-death list.

### Taking a break before Jad

The TzTok-Jad section of the strategy page is explicit. **Do not log out after wave 62.** You spawn in the middle and get one action before Jad appears. Instead, log out after clearing **wave 61**, set your quick prayer to Protect from Magic, and log back in. You will face the two Ket-Zeks with time to set up. Don't click through the chat dialogue; turn on your quick prayer, and that starts the wave.

## Logging Out Mid-Run

| What you do | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Click Log Out once | Two red messages appear. Finish the current wave and you log out. The next wave starts when you log back in. |
| Click Log Out twice, or log out after 10 seconds out of combat with monsters still alive | Progress is saved, but you repeat the wave you were on. |
| Log back in | You start in the middle of the cave, and the wave doesn't begin until you act, so set your prayer and run to your safespot first. |

The Fight Cave page notes that logging out between waves is a free way to regenerate Hitpoints: find a safespot, request a log out, finish the wave and let your HP come back. A regen bracelet doubles natural regeneration to 2 Hitpoints a minute.

## Hardcore, Ultimate and Group Ironmen

HCIM: the Fight Cave is a safe death

The TzHaar Fight Cave is on the wiki's list of safe deaths for Hardcore Ironmen, and the Fight Cave page says it "does not apply to standard Hardcore Ironman players, who can always safely die in the Fight Caves". When you die you respawn outside with your stats restored, all of your equipment and inventory, and the Tokkul for the waves you completed. That makes it a place where a hardcore can practise prayer switching against a boss that hits 97 without risking status. See our [HCIM guide](https://ironman.guide/hcim).

Two details from the fire cape page apply to every account. The cape is **not** awarded if you and Jad die at the same time, even though your boss log counts the kill, because dying removes you from the cave as a failure. Since 2019, though, reaching 0 Hitpoints as you win no longer kills you once you are taken outside.

- **Ultimate ironmen:** the Fight Cave page says a death in the cave does **not** lose the contents of your Item Retrieval Service. See the [UIM guide](https://ironman.guide/uim) .
- **Hardcore Group Ironmen:** the cave is on the wiki's list of activities that are **unsafe** for HCGIM. A death costs the group one of its shared lives. The elite Karamja Diary's daily resurrection is the exception and does not cost a life. See the [GIM guide](https://ironman.guide/gim) .
- **Hardcore Worlds** (a new mode for main accounts, launching 4 November 2026) use the HCGIM death rules, so the Fight Cave is unsafe there too. As of October 2026 this has no effect on normal Hardcore Ironmen.

## Rewards

**Tokkul** is paid for every wave you complete, even if you die. The formula is N × (N + 1), where N is waves completed, plus 4,000 more for killing Jad. A full clear is 8,032 Tokkul, doubled to 16,064 with the elite Karamja Diary.

**The fire cape** is guaranteed on every completion, so you can earn more than one. TzHaar-Mej-Jal takes spare capes in exchange for either 8,000 Tokkul or a 1/200 roll at the pet. The game asks for a second confirmation before trading one in for Tokkul.

**TzRek-Jad,** the pet:

| How | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Kill Jad off task | 1/200 |
| Kill Jad on a TzHaar Slayer task | 1/100 |
| Trade in the fire cape | 1/200 |
| Kill plus cape trade, off task | About 1/101 overall |
| Kill plus cape trade, on task | About 1/67 overall |

If your inventory is full and you already have a follower out, the pet goes to Probita in East Ardougne instead. Our [drop calculator](https://ironman.guide/drop-calculator) shows how dry a given number of kills is.

## Jad as a Slayer Task

Buy **Hot Stuff** for 100 Slayer points, and Duradel, Nieve and Chaeldar can assign TzHaar. When you get a TzHaar task you can take 1 TzTok-Jad instead, or 2 with the Master Combat Achievements and 3 with Grandmaster. Take the Jad option if you want the cape: a normal TzHaar task tops out at 199 kills, which only lasts until wave 48.

On task:

- Your **Slayer helmet (i) or imbued black mask** bonus applies to every monster in the cave, not just Jad.
- **Slayer XP:** waves 1-62 give 11,520, Jad gives 25,250, and the four healers 60 each, for 36,770 to 37,010 per run.
- **The pet chance doubles** to 1/100.
- **Leaving the cave** in any way, including dying, clears the task. The TzTok-Jad page notes that this does not reset your task streak, so it doubles as a free skip.

Our [Slayer guide](https://ironman.guide/articles/osrs-ironman-slayer-guide) covers when Hot Stuff is worth the points in your unlock order.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you get a fire cape in OSRS?

Complete the TzHaar Fight Cave in Mor Ul Rek under the Karamja volcano: survive 62 waves of TzHaar on your own, then kill TzTok-Jad on wave 63. Every completion awards a fire cape and 8,032 Tokkul. There are no level requirements, but the OSRS Wiki suggests around 61 Ranged, 70 Hitpoints and 43 Prayer for an early ironman using a rune crossbow, and most players use Ranged for the whole run. The cape is not awarded if you and Jad die at the same time.

### What stats do ironmen need for the Fight Caves?

The wiki's early-game iron setup suggests 61+ Ranged, 70+ Hitpoints, 43+ Prayer, 50+ Defence, Attack and Strength, and 56+ Magic for blood spells. Its general Ranged recommendation is higher: 75 Ranged, 75 Hitpoints, 60 Prayer and 70 Defence. 43 Prayer is the one you cannot skip, because Protect from Missiles and Protect from Magic carry the whole run and the Jad fight.

### Is the Fight Cave safe for hardcore ironmen?

Yes. The TzHaar Fight Cave is on the OSRS Wiki's list of safe deaths for Hardcore Ironmen, and the Fight Cave page says standard Hardcore Ironmen can always safely die there. You respawn outside with your equipment, inventory and the Tokkul for completed waves; only the supplies you used are gone. It is not safe for Hardcore Group Ironmen, where a death costs the group a life, unless it is the once-a-day elite Karamja Diary resurrection.

### What is the best fire cape setup for an ironman?

Ranged is the standard. The wiki's early-game iron setup is a rune crossbow with broad bolts, or Karil's crossbow with bolt racks, plus diamond bolts (e) for Jad, Ava's accumulator, a book of law, Barrows gloves and a mix of d'hide, Void or Karil's armour. Irons within boosting range of 74 Fletching and 72 Hunter should use the hunters' sunlight crossbow for the waves. Once you have a toxic blowpipe from Zulrah, the wiki calls it the top single weapon for the entire run.

### What is the TzRek-Jad pet drop rate?

Killing TzTok-Jad has a 1/200 chance of the TzRek-Jad pet, or 1/100 if you kill it on a TzHaar Slayer task. Each completion also gives a fire cape that you can hand to TzHaar-Mej-Jal for another 1/200 roll, which the wiki puts at roughly 1/101 overall off task and about 1/67 on task.

### Can you log out in the Fight Caves?

Yes. Click Log Out once and the game will log you out as soon as the current wave is cleared, then start the next wave when you log back in. Clicking Log Out twice logs you out straight away, but you repeat the wave you were on. The wiki warns not to log out after wave 62, because you only get one action before Jad spawns; log out after wave 61 with your quick prayer set to Protect from Magic instead.

### What should an ironman spend Tokkul on?

Death runes from TzHaar-Mej-Roh's Rune Store, gems including uncut onyx for an amulet of fury, onyx bolt tips, and obsidian equipment such as the obsidian cape, toktz-ket-xil shield and obsidian throwing rings. Wear Karamja gloves for the discount: an uncut onyx drops from 300,000 to 260,000 Tokkul. The wiki notes that ironmen commonly raise Tokkul by selling chaos runes to the rune store.
