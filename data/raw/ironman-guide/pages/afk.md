Rows tagged Iron have account-build notes — tap them.

## Choosing AFK Methods on an Ironman

On a main account, AFK training is a trade: you accept slower XP in exchange for attention spent elsewhere, and the GE turns whatever you produce back into gp. An ironman doesn't get that conversion — which flips the calculus. The best iron AFK methods are the ones where the *output* is something your account was going to grind anyway: cannonballs for Slayer, Zulrah's scales for the blowpipe, mahogany planks for Construction, monkfish and anglerfish for your PvM supply bank. Run those during meetings, TV, or other games, and entire bottlenecks disappear from your account's critical path.

A useful pattern is pairing producers with consumers. AFK woodcutting at redwoods feeds forester's campfires for Firemaking and redwood shields for Fletching; daeyalt essence mined AFK makes your future blood-rune runecrafting 50% faster; gem drops from gemstone crabs become the bolt tips you cut AFK at a bank. The table's "Banks" column exists to make those chains visible. For methods that generate actual coins — which is a separate problem on an iron — see the [ironman money making guide](https://ironman.guide/money-making).

Two cautions. First, "AFK" never means unattended — you still need to click within the logout window, and hardcore ironmen should treat AFK combat with real care (Vyrewatch Sentinels and dark crabs both punish inattention; see the [HCIM guide](https://ironman.guide/hcim)). Second, don't let AFK-ability justify a method you don't need: burning redwoods you could have fletched, or offering bones at a gilded altar when the Chaos Temple would double them, is convenience paid for with your own gathering time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most AFK training method in OSRS?

Four methods hit the 30-minute ceiling: infected roots (80 Woodcutting), Stranglewood fishing (63 Fishing), the Blast Furnace pump (30 Strength), and crewmate-only salvaging (Sailing). Nothing goes longer because the idle logout timer ends the session — which is why the wiki's own AFK guide caps every listed method at 30:00.

### What is the best AFK combat training in OSRS?

Sand, ammonite, and gemstone crabs give around 100k XP/hr with roughly 10 minutes of AFK per reset at any combat level. From 60+ combat, Nightmare Zone with absorption potions stretches to 20-minute AFK windows at 105k–150k XP/hr, and the points buy herb boxes — a significant herb source for ironmen.

### Which AFK methods should an ironman prioritise?

Pick methods that bank resources your account needs, not just XP: smithing cannonballs for Slayer, sacred eels for Zulrah's scales, amethyst for darts and broad bolts, mahogany logs into plank make for Construction, and Vyrewatch Sentinels for blood shards. On an ironman the output column matters more than the XP column.

### Can you train AFK in free-to-play?

Yes. Yew trees, shooting stars, barronite mining, cooking, smithing one-bar items, spinning, forester's campfires, enchanting jewellery, and splashing all work in F2P — use the F2P filter in the table above to see every option.

### Are the listed XP rates realistic?

They're ceilings. Following the wiki's methodology, rates assume level 99 with the full skilling outfit and optimal gear. At lower levels or without the outfit you'll see less — but the AFK durations and click counts hold at any level.
