# OSRS Sailing Guide 2026: 1-99 Sailing for Ironmen, Ships, Ports & Quests

OSRS Sailing guide for ironmen: how to start, ship upgrades, 1-99 training with wiki XP rates, Barracuda Trials, courier routes, every Sailing quest and unlock.

21 min read·Updated September 17, 2026·Difficulty: **

Sailing came out on **19 November 2025** and is the only skill exclusive to Old School RuneScape. It is also the skill that has changed the most since launch: Jagex has shipped balance passes almost monthly in 2026, so this guide states every figure as it appears on the OSRS Wiki **as of 17 September 2026** and says so when the wiki itself flags a number as provisional.

For an ironman the point of Sailing is not the level. It is the islands. Twenty-seven of them are reachable only by boat, and they hold things you cannot get anywhere else: lead and nickel for boat parts, camphor, ironwood and rosewood logs, coral and coloured crabs for unique potions, squid beaks for a self-made blowpipe, rubium for incendiary cannonballs, four runite rocks, frost dragons, and Wyrmscraig with the best mid-game Crafting method in the game. Deep sea trawling adds halibut and marlin. Salvage feeds planks, nails and cannonballs back into your ship. Treat the skill as an unlock tree and the training becomes a means to an end.

## How to start

Talk to **Will and Anne on the Port Sarim docks** to begin **Pandemonium**. It has no requirements, is Novice and short, and teaches you to sail, dock and do the basic tasks. Rewards are your first boat, a **raft**, a **captain's log**, 1 quest point and **300 Sailing XP**, with another 100 XP when you deliver the cargo. After the quest you board the raft at the gangplank on the east dock of The Pandemonium.

Three things unlock immediately: trimming sails, ocean encounters, courier tasks and sea charting, and bounty tasks later at level 30. Gusts of wind appear around your sails while moving; trim them within 12 seconds for a speed boost and a small XP drop, 10.5 XP per trim on the starting raft. Always trim.

Keep the raft forever

The wiki is explicit: a few sea charting tasks can only be reached on a raft, because skiffs and sloops do not fit. It is also the cheapest boat to teleport around through a shipwright. Do not sell or scrap it when you buy the skiff.

## Ships and upgrades

Boats come from a shipwright at a large port. The level to own each boat is not boostable.

| Skiff | 15 | Small (2x5) | 7 | 5 | 2 | 15,000 |

Own all three. The skiff is required for Barracuda Trials, only the raft reaches some charting spots, and the sloop is the upgrade for salvaging, trawling and boat combat. The wiki warns the sloop is "hugely expensive to upgrade or retrieve" and cannot fit certain small spaces.

Skip the middle tiers

The wiki's ironman guide says intermediate boat upgrades give only minor speed or stat gains and can be skipped to save resources, and that armour and health upgrades do not speed up XP unless you are doing bounty tasks. Spend your planks on the parts in the table above, in the order the trials need them.

## Training 1-99

The wiki's recommended fast route is quests, sea charting and courier tasks to 30, then the three Barracuda Trials: The Tempor Tantrum at 30, The Jubbly Jive at 55 and The Gwenith Glide at 72. Everything else is a lower-intensity substitute. Two things stack on top of whatever you do: trimming sails, and from 73 the crystal extractor.

### Levels 1-30: quests, charting and courier tasks

The wiki's own progression:

- **1-4:** Pandemonium.
- **4-12:** Courier tasks between Port Sarim and The Pandemonium while sailing to sea charting locations. Only generic and spyglass charting tasks are possible yet. At 5, Land's End tasks pay slightly more but are further away. At 7 you can hold two tasks; two deliveries between Port Sarim and The Pandemonium effectively double the XP. At 10, Musa Point opens.
- **12-14:** Prying Times (30 Smithing, The Knight's Sword, one free task slot) for the crowbar, which unlocks crowbar charting tasks.
- **14-22:** Courier tasks and crowbar charting.
- **22-24:** Current Affairs (10 Fishing) for the current duck and its charting tasks.
- **24-30:** Courier tasks and current duck charting.

Sea charting is one-off XP for exploring, with bonus XP for finishing an area, and it is the better of the two early methods. The wiki puts it at around **10,000 XP per hour at level 22**, most of it from completing regions, and says that charting alone, stopping only for the quests, is enough to reach 45 for Troubled Tortugans. Fully charting an area also unlocks rapids in it, which speed you up if you have an iron helm or better. Charting every ocean needs 78 Sailing, boostable from 76, and unlocks horizon's lure: an ale that boosts all Sailing XP by 2.5% when kept in a keg.

Courier tasks pay more for longer distances, and tasks whose cargo sits at a different port than the notice board pay more again. Accepting a task elsewhere and teleporting to the pickup raises XP per hour. Every completion also gives a port coin bag or a port reward bag; reward bags carry ship-combat and shipbuilding supplies including sawmill coupons.

### Levels 30-55: The Tempor Tantrum

Buy a skiff at 15 and fit an iron helm and oak mast and linen sails; those are the requirements to enter. Each trial has Swordfish, Shark and Marlin ranks with tighter time limits. Every completion gives a flat XP amount regardless of time, the first completion of each rank gives bonus XP, and beating the target time on the Shark rank of each trial is the one that matters, because it awards the facility item.

| Rank | XP per trial | XP per hour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Swordfish | 634 | 19,342 |
| Shark | 1,103 | 21,938 |
| Marlin | 1,907 | 24,519 |

These are the wiki's figures, computed from its per-lap XP and target times plus ten seconds. Rewards are the stormy key, Ralph's fabric roll, the barrel stand (build a keg) and, from the Shark rank, the whirlpool surprise ale: a +3 visible Sailing boost that also gives an invisible +2 when the keg is on board.

Do Crab Quest at 40 (30 Fishing, 10,000 XP) and Troubled Tortugans at 45 as soon as you meet its other requirements:

It pays 10,000 Sailing XP, opens The Great Conch and unlocks the Summer Shore courier route.

### Levels 55-72: The Jubbly Jive

Needs a mithril helm (55 Sailing, 47 Construction, 8 mithril bars) and an inoculation station (40 Sailing, 37 Construction, partial Zogre Flesh Eaters).

| Rank | XP per trial | XP per hour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Swordfish | 2,392 | 64,746 |
| Shark | 4,270 | 80,905 |
| Marlin | 8,176 | 88,923 |

The Shark rank, under 3:00, awards the captured wind mote for the wind catcher. Complete The Red Reef at 52 (48 Smithing, Troubled Tortugans) for 15,000 XP and 2 quest points, and Fallen From Grace at 62 for 12,500 XP. Fallen From Grace wants a raft or skiff, not a sloop, and there is no bank on Wyrmscraig until it is done; the [Wyrmscraig guide](https://ironman.guide/articles/wyrmscraig-ironman-guide) covers the fight.

If you would rather not race, the **Rellekka courier route** opens at 62: check the Rellekka board for Aldarin, Sunset Coast or Port Roberts deliveries, fill remaining slots down the Varlamore and Kourend coasts, and summon the boat to Aldarin with a teleport focus. The wiki estimates about **66,000 XP per hour** with only Rellekka unlocked and about **99,000** once Etceteria is available at 65, and notes rates depend heavily on which tasks the boards offer. At 70 with Song of the Elves, the Prifddinas route is estimated at 71,000 to 77,000.

Courier figures are unretested

The wiki says every courier route estimate was extrapolated from testing done before courier XP was raised by 10% on 3 June 2026, and that none has been independently retested since. Treat them as ballpark numbers.

### Levels 72-99: The Gwenith Glide

Needs Regicide and an adamant keel (66 Sailing, 62 Construction, 74 Smithing, 50 adamantite bars).

| Rank | XP per trial | XP per hour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Swordfish | 4,100 | 113,538 |
| Shark | 9,315 | 144,543 |
| Marlin | 19,410 | 184,369 |

With a crystal extractor harvested during laps the wiki puts the Marlin rank at about **198,000 XP per hour**, and with a rosewood hull at 93 Sailing and 84 Construction (both boostable) it says **200,000 or higher**. The Shark rank, under 3:42, awards the heart of Ithell for the extractor.

The **Lunar Isle courier route** from 76 is the relaxed alternative: accept every round-trip task on the Lunar Isle board (Aldarin 12,671 XP, Deepfin Point 9,092, Civitas illa Fortis 7,381, Port Roberts 5,851, Prifddinas 5,491, Piscatoris 3,150), add Prifddinas round trips, fill slots at the furthest pickup and sail back. At 84 you hold five tasks. The wiki's estimate is **130,000 to 160,000 XP per hour** depending on board luck, upgrades and the extractor.

### Crystal extractor

Built at 73 Sailing and 67 Construction, it makes wind motes, and harvesting one gives 250 Sailing XP roughly every 63 seconds. That is 15,000 XP per hour used perfectly and about 10,000 realistically, on top of anything else you are doing. On a skiff, put it in the slot to the right of the helm so you can harvest without leaving the helm; on a sloop, above and to the left of the helm, or next to your main salvaging hook.

### Lower-input alternatives

**Shipwreck salvaging** from 15, much better after the salvaging station at 42. Jagex's own published tests, with four-deckhandiness crew and no extractor, were 35,000 to 40,000 XP per hour at 97 Sailing sorting casually while two crewmates worked two dragon hooks, about 85,000 actively multitasking, and 105,000 to 110,000 at 87 with rune hooks salvaging and sorting actively. Tick manipulation roughly doubles to 2.5 times that. The full method, wreck tiers and loot are in the [shipwreck salvaging guide](https://ironman.guide/articles/shipwreck-salvaging-guide).

**Deep sea trawling** trains Fishing at the same time. The wiki marks all of its estimates as provisional and incomplete, but the recorded ones on a sloop with two nets run from 7,000 to 10,000 Sailing XP per hour on a giant krill shoal with rope nets (56 Sailing, 69 Fishing) up to 57,000 to 60,000 on halibut or bluefin shoals with cotton nets (84 Sailing, 83 or 87 Fishing), the top figures including extractor XP. In May 2026 Jagex roughly doubled trawling XP and cut the gather interval from 4 ticks to 3.

**Bounty tasks** are for drops, not XP: the wiki says their XP was cut 25% in April 2026 and the task lengths were raised about five times in June with XP rebalanced to match. All bounty tasks can be done at 80.

## Boosts

You can boost to recruit crew, chart and build parts and facilities, but never to dock, own a boat, or start a quest. An active facility built on a boost (cannons, hooks, nets, chum stations) only works while the boost is held; support facilities such as the cargo hold, salvaging station and extractor keep working. Ales go in a keg for their second effect.

## Ironman-specific tips

- **Plank supply is the real bottleneck.** The wiki's ironman guide says the hardest part is gathering planks, nails, cannonballs and keel parts, and that ship size multiplies the cost per tier. Set up Razmire Builders Merchants after Shades of Mort'ton, use Managing Miscellania for teak and mahogany, spend the sawmill coupons from early events and quests, and keep forestry sawmill vouchers for the high-level logs.
- **Runite bars without 85 Mining:** offer rune platelegs, which are buyable in shops, at the Nest (nice) in Aldarin to convert them into runite bars. The eternal brazier needs six.
- **Sort your salvage.** Mains drop it on deck for XP; the wiki says ironmen should sort as often as possible, because low-tier salvage carries useful early resources, sawmill coupons, and a chance at the sailors' amulet from any tier. Each wreck tier also has a 1/3,000 museum find worth kudos and an antique lamp.
- **Death and capsizing.** Dying on a boat puts your gravestone at the last gangplank you sailed from, not the mooring point. Capsizing sends you there with your inventory, is safe for Hardcore Ironmen, and does not cure poison, venom or disease. Either way, cargo crates, bounty items, salvage and trawled fish in the hold are lost; cannonballs, repair kits and empty fish crates are kept.
- **Group Ironmen** can use facilities on group members' ships without building them, as long as they have the Sailing level.
- **Bounty tasks feed your boat.** Orcas drop dragon metal sheets and dragon nails, and frost dragons and lava strykewyrms drop sheets at improved rates, so the dragon-tier parts are farmable rather than bought.
- **The pet is Soup.** Sorting any salvage has a small chance to give it, and deep sea trawling can also give the Heron.

## Salvaging in one paragraph

Buy a skiff at 15, build a bronze salvaging hook and a cargo hold, park next to a small shipwreck and operate the hook until the wreck deactivates, then move to the next one. Wrecks in an area share a spawn pool of about six spots with up to four active, so the official salvaging worlds keep nearby wrecks respawning. Move up a tier every time you unlock one, sort at a salvaging station, and put crew on the second hook once you have a sloop. Tiers, hooks, loot tables and the rune-versus-dragon-hook numbers are in the [shipwreck salvaging guide](https://ironman.guide/articles/shipwreck-salvaging-guide).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you start Sailing in OSRS?

Complete the Pandemonium quest by talking to Will and Anne on the Port Sarim docks. It has no requirements, teaches the basics, and rewards a raft, a captain's log and 300 Sailing XP, plus 100 more when you deliver the cargo. After the quest you board the raft from the gangplank on the east dock of The Pandemonium and can start courier tasks and sea charting straight away.

### What is the fastest way to train Sailing?

From level 30 the Barracuda Trials are the fastest repeatable XP: The Tempor Tantrum at 30, The Jubbly Jive at 55 and The Gwenith Glide at 72. The wiki's figures run from about 19,000 XP per hour on the first trial's Swordfish rank to about 184,000 on the Gwenith Glide Marlin rank, and roughly 198,000 with a crystal extractor. Before 30, sea charting and courier tasks are the best XP, with sea charting around 10,000 XP per hour at level 22.

### What are the Sailing XP rates by level?

Roughly: sea charting and courier tasks under 10,000 to 20,000 XP per hour before level 30; The Tempor Tantrum 19,000 to 25,000 from 30; The Jubbly Jive 65,000 to 89,000 from 55; the Rellekka courier route about 66,000 from 62 and 99,000 from 65; The Gwenith Glide 114,000 to 184,000 from 72; the Lunar Isle courier route 130,000 to 160,000 from 76. The wiki marks its courier figures as estimates that have not been retested since courier XP was raised 10 percent in June 2026.

### Which quests require or give Sailing XP?

Seven quests give Sailing XP: Pandemonium 300, Prying Times 800 at 12 Sailing, Current Affairs 1,400 at 22, Crab Quest 10,000 at 40, Troubled Tortugans 10,000 at 45, The Red Reef 15,000 at 52 and Fallen From Grace 12,500 at 62. That is 50,000 XP in total. None of the Sailing level requirements are boostable.

### What does Sailing unlock for an ironman?

Islands and resources you cannot get elsewhere: lead and nickel for boat parts, camphor, ironwood and rosewood logs, coral and crab potions, squid for blowpipes, rubium for incendiary cannonballs, and islands like The Great Conch at 45, Charred Island at 60, Wyrmscraig at 62 with Golem Crafting and the Mad Angel, and Grimstone at 87 with frost dragons and double-speed cannonball smelting. Deep sea trawling also adds halibut and marlin, which the wiki calls the best single-bite food in the game.

### Why does Wyrmscraig need 62 Sailing?

Wyrmscraig is an island you can only reach by mooring, and mooring points have hard Sailing requirements that cannot be boosted. 62 is its docking level, and Fallen From Grace, the quest that unlocks Golem Crafting, the Mad Angel and Mortimer, also requires 62 Sailing unboosted along with 60 Crafting, 47 Runecraft and 53 Mining.

### Is Sailing boostable?

Partly. You can boost Sailing to recruit crewmates, do sea charting and build higher-tier facilities and core boat parts, but you cannot boost to dock at mooring points or to meet boat or quest level requirements. Boosts include the Sailing cape at +1, kraken ink stout and Perildance bitter at +1, Trawler's trust at +2, whirlpool surprise at +3 and horizon's lure at +4, plus a yellow spicy stew for 0 to 5 either way.
