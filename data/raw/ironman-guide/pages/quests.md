# OSRS Ironman Quest Order Guide

215 quests and miniquests organized into 6 phases with ironman-specific reasons for each. 60 milestone quests highlighted. For ironmen, quest XP rewards are the primary way to skip early grinds — there is no GE to buy supplies or catch up. Getting the order right saves dozens of hours. Requirements and XP values sourced from the OSRS Wiki, verified October 2026.

## Why Quest Order Matters for Ironmen

- XP Efficiency: Quest XP rewards skip entire weeks of early grinding — Waterfall Quest alone grants 30 Attack and Strength from level 1.
- Unlock Chains: Some quests gate entire training methods. You cannot train Herblore without Druidic Ritual; you cannot use fairy rings without starting Fairytale II.
- Transportation: Teleports and travel networks (spirit trees, gnome gliders, fairy rings, Ectophial) save massive time when unlocked early.
- Item Gating: Ava's device, proselyte armour, barrows gloves, and dragon scimitar are all quest rewards — not GE purchases.
- Passive Resources: Managing Miscellania, disease-free herb patches, and Fossil Island birdhouses all require quests and provide passive gains forever.
