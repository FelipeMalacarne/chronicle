# OSRS Ironman Gear Progression 2026

What to wield and wear at every stage of an ironman account, and where each piece comes from. The tables below mirror the OSRS Wiki's Ironman Guide progression for melee, ranged and magic; the interactive section underneath adds stats, sprites and upgrade paths for 90+ items.

Progression order and sources follow the OSRS Wiki's Ironman Guide for [Melee](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ironman_Guide/Melee), [Ranged](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ironman_Guide/Ranged) and [Magic](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ironman_Guide/Magic), checked 17 September 2026. Items are listed in the order the wiki recommends working through them. Levels shown are what the wiki states; where the wiki gives none, the cell is blank rather than guessed.
