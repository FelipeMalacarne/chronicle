## Fire Weakness (Important)

Since May 2024, Zulrah has a **50% weakness to fire** — fire spells are 50% more accurate and deal 50% more damage.

**Powered staves (Trident, Sanguinesti, Tumekens shadow) do NOT benefit from this.** Only standard spellbook fire spells do.

What this means for gear choices:

- The weakness itself is where the damage comes from — fire spells get the full 50% bonus regardless of what you hold
- **Twinflame staff** + **Tome of fire** casting fire spells is very strong — it exploits the weakness, fires two spells per cast, and the Tome of fire adds a further 10% damage against NPCs
- **Harmonised nightmare staff** + Tome of fire is BIS for fire spell exploitation (4-tick fire spells)
- Trident / Sanguinesti / Shadow are still top weapons overall due to raw stats, but they **miss the fire bonus entirely**

## Rotation Prayers

| Form | Colour | Zulrah Attacks With | Your Prayer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Serpentine | Green | Ranged | **Protect from Missiles** |
| Tanzanite | Blue | Magic (mostly) + Ranged | **Protect from Magic** |

## Drop Rates

**2 rolls per kill**. Each roll: **1/1,024** at a unique = effective **~1/512 per kill**.

| Item | Rate | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tanzanite fang | ~1/512 | Essential — toxic blowpipe |
| Magic fang | ~1/512 | Essential — trident of the swamp upgrade |
| Serpentine visage | ~1/512 | Important — venom immunity helm |
| Scales | 100-299/kill | Essential — fuel for blowpipe + trident |

| Target | Expected Kills | 50th Percentile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Any unique | ~171 | ~118 |
| Both fangs | ~768 | ~628 |
| All 3 uniques | ~939 | ~807 |

Use the [dry calculator](https://ironman.guide/drop-calculator) to check your luck.

## When to Stop

- **Both fangs + visage** = done with uniques
- **50k+ scales** needed to fuel blowpipe and trident through raids (~250 kills just for scales)
- If you have **Bowfa already** , blowpipe is less critical — mainly farm for magic fang and scales
- Come back for more scales whenever you run low
