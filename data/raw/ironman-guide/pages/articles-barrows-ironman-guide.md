## Gear (Wiki Priority Order)

Use the best item you own from each row. Magic is the only style used on 5 of 6 brothers. Switch to ranged for Ahrim only.

All six brothers are **50% weak to air spells** — wind spells are 50% more accurate and deal 50% more damage against them, which is why a Twinflame staff double-casting wind spells punches far above its raw stats here.

## Kill Order

The wiki-verified fastest order:

**Dharok → Ahrim → Verac → Torag → Karil → Guthan**

| # | Brother | Prayer | Attack With |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Dharok | Protect from Melee | Magic |
| 2 | Ahrim | Protect from Magic | Ranged switch |
| 3 | Verac | Protect from Melee | Magic |
| 4 | Torag | Protect from Melee | Magic |
| 5 | Karil | Protect from Missiles | Magic |
| 6 | Guthan | Protect from Melee | Magic |

Dharok first because his max hit scales with his missing HP — most dangerous. Ahrim second because he hits hard with magic. The rest are low threat.

## Tunnel — Target 880 Reward Potential (~87%)

After killing all 6 brothers, dig on any mound. Kill the tunnel brother plus small monsters to reach **880 reward potential** (displays as ~87% in game).

- 6 brothers + 1 giant crypt spider + 1 skeleton + 1 crypt spider = 880 RP
- Or: 6 brothers + 4 bloodworms = 876 RP (close enough)

At 881+ RP, bolt racks unlock and dilute your rune drops. Stay at or below 880.

## Drop Rates

**7 rolls per chest** with all 6 brothers killed. Each roll: **1/102** chance at a Barrows item.

| Target | Expected Chests |
| --- | --- |
| Any Barrows item | ~15 |
| Specific piece | ~350 |
| Full Guthans (4 pcs) | ~730 |

## Tips

- **Morytania Hard Diary** gives 50% more runes per chest
- **Kite melee brothers** around pillars in their crypt to take zero damage
- **Ferox Enclave** (Ring of Dueling) gives free full restore between trips
- **Strange old lockpick** lets you skip the puzzle doors in the tunnels
