# Hardcore Ironman Guide

Safe training methods, dangers to avoid, and strategies to protect your red helmet

## One Life, No Respawns

Hardcore Ironman mode is permanent. One death and your status is gone forever. This guide focuses on minimizing risk while still making efficient progress.

## HCIM-Specific Tips

### Top Priorities for HCIM

1. **43 Prayer ASAP** - Protection prayers are your lifeline. Prioritize blue dragon bones at Taverley dungeon (safe with Iban Blast).
2. **Ring of Life** - Always wear one when doing any risky content. Ironmen have no Grand Exchange - craft a diamond ring (43 Crafting) and enchant it with Lvl-4 Enchant (57 Magic). Note it will not trigger above level 30 Wilderness.
3. **Emergency Teleports** - Keep teleport tabs in your inventory at all times. Royal seed pod (from Monkey Madness II) works up to level 30 wilderness, but teleports are delayed inside the Revenant Caves and wilderness boss lairs until you complete the hard Wilderness diary.
4. **Use XP Lamps on Prayer** - After 77 Herblore, put all lamps and books into Prayer to minimize dangerous bone grinding.

### Dangers to Avoid

#### High Risk Activities

- Wilderness content (PKers, multi-combat)
- Wintertodt and Hallowed Sepulchre (unsafe deaths - see below)
- Temple Trekking (combat events hit hard)
- Demonic Gorillas (high damage)
- Solo bossing without an escape teleport

#### Common Death Causes

- Disconnections during combat
- Forgetting to restore prayer
- Misclicking into multi-combat
- Aggressive NPCs while AFKing
- Poison/venom without antipoison

### Safe Training Methods

#### Truly Safe Deaths (Status Kept)

- Nightmare Zone and Pest Control (every death is safe)
- TzHaar Fight Cave and the Inferno (both explicitly safe)
- Chambers of Xeric
- Castle Wars, Soul Wars, LMS, Barbarian Assault
- Random events and your player-owned house

#### Combat

- Sand Crabs / Ammonite Crabs (AFK, minimal damage)
- Slayer with protection prayers active
- Learn Fight Caves early - dying there only costs supplies

#### Prayer

- Blue dragons in Taverley Dungeon (safespot with magic)
- Green dragons in the Myths' Guild basement (no Wilderness needed)
- Ensouled heads with Arceuus spellbook
- Forthos Dungeon red dragons (safespottable)

#### Looks Safe - It Is Not

- Wintertodt: since the Oct 2024 rework, warmth damage scales with your Firemaking level (the old 10 HP trick is dead) - hitting 0% warmth is instant death and removes Hardcore status
- Hallowed Sepulchre: an unsafe death that ends your Hardcore run - items go to a 25k-fee deathbank that is wiped if you die again before claiming
- Tempoross: the boss deals no damage, but dying there to poison or venom still counts as an unsafe death
- The Blood Moon Rises (June 2026): quest deaths respawn you at a checkpoint with your items kept, but a Hardcore still loses status on any death inside the quest
- Sailing: capsizing is safe, but dying aboard a ship is a normal unsafe death

### 2026 Death-Rule Changes

#### The Blood Moon Rises (30 June 2026)

Deaths inside the Vampyrium sections respawn you at a checkpoint with everything kept, and the Drakan fights hand out unlimited supplies, so the quest looks safe. It is not: the OSRS Wiki is explicit that Hardcore Ironmen still lose their status if they die at any point during the quest, and the Ver Sinhaza wave section is a normal death with a gravestone. Over-prepare for every fight and bank a spare Drakan's medallion.

#### Trouver rework (17 June 2026)

Untradeables now sit in three tiers on a PvP death in the Wilderness. This changes what you get back, not your status: a death to a player in the Wilderness is still an unsafe death.

- **No combat bonus** (Graceful and other skilling outfits): never lost any more, at any Wilderness level.
- **Some combat bonus** : breaks and is repaired at Perdu for the normal fee, no parchment needed. Includes the fire cape, imbued god capes, Ava's assembler, every defender up to avernic, halos, Barbarian Assault hats and the penance skirt.
- **Strong combat bonus** : needs one Trouver parchment (18 LMS points, or a Wilderness Slayer Cave drop) plus 500,000 coins at Perdu, once - the lock now survives death. Below level 20 Wilderness a locked item breaks for the normal fee; above 20 it is mangled and costs 500,000 to fix. Left unlocked, these are still lost above 20 Wilderness. Includes all void pieces, the infernal cape, fighter torso, rune pouch and divine rune pouch, Dizana's quiver and the ancient sceptres.

#### Fallen From Grace on Wyrmscraig (29 July 2026)

The quest ends with a level 270 Mad Angel and there is no bank or teleport on the island until it is complete. Sail in with boss supplies, not just quest items, and bring a raft or skiff because a sloop will not fit the cave. See the [Wyrmscraig guide](https://ironman.guide/articles/wyrmscraig-ironman-guide).
