There are {{Globals|quests p2p}} [[pay-to-play]] [[quests]] in ''[[Old School RuneScape]]'', offering a total of {{SCP|Quest|{{Globals|quest points p2p}}|link=yes}}. Together with the [[Quests/Free-to-play|free-to-play quests]], there is a total of {{Globals|quests}} quests for {{SCP|Quest|{{Globals|quest points}}|link=yes}}.

Below is a list of all [[Quests/Members|members-only quests]]. While the listed difficulties and lengths are the averages of each quest, these can still vary for every player.<noinclude> Some recommendations presented in quest guides may only represent a single point of view out of many possible strategies. Balance these walkthroughs with your own abilities and your knowledge of ''Old School RuneScape''.
