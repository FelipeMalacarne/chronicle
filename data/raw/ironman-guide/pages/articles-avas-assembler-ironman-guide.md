# OSRS Ava's Assembler: How to Get It, Requirements, Stats and the Ironman Route

How to get Ava's assembler in OSRS: Dragon Slayer II, Vorkath's head (1/50, guaranteed on kill 50), 75 mithril arrows and the accumulator or 4,999 coins at Ava in Draynor Manor. Stats, what it saves, and the ironman route.

20 min read·Updated November 1, 2026·Difficulty: ***

Ava's devices are the cape-slot backpacks that catch your arrows and bolts after you fire them. On an ironman that matters more than the stats: every arrow you lose is a bar you smelted and a log you fletched, and the assembler makes each arrow last five shots on average. Getting there takes one easy quest at 30 Ranged, a 75-arrow upgrade at 50, and then Dragon Slayer II plus a Vorkath's head.

Everything here was checked against the OSRS Wiki in October 2026.

## Ava's Assembler Quick Facts

| Question | Short answer |
| --- | --- |
| **How do you get Ava's assembler?** | Take **Vorkath's head**, **75 mithril arrows** and either **Ava's accumulator or 4,999 coins** to Ava in Draynor Manor. |
| **Requirements** | **Dragon Slayer II** completed (you need it to fight Vorkath) and **70 Ranged** to wear the assembler. |
| **Vorkath's head drop rate** | **1/50** per kill, and **guaranteed on your 50th Vorkath kill**. |
| **Stats** | **+8 Ranged attack, +2 Ranged Strength**, +1 stab, slash and crush defence, +8 Magic and +2 Ranged defence. Second-best cape-slot Ranged attack after Dizana's quiver. |
| **Ammo saved** | **80%** of shots are recovered. The other 20% break on impact, so nothing lands on the floor and each arrow lasts about 5 shots. |
| **What stops it working** | Metal torso armour, including Ahrim's robetop. Interference table below. |
| **Lost it?** | Lost Property shops stock a replacement; the wiki notes Vorkath's head is required for that purchase. A Masori crafting kit turns it into the cosmetic Masori assembler with identical stats. |

The ironman route in four steps

1. **30 Ranged:** do Animal Magnetism for Ava's attractor. Finish it with 50+ Ranged and you get the accumulator straight away.
2. **50 Ranged:** give Ava 75 steel arrows plus the attractor or 999 coins for Ava's accumulator.
3. **Dragon Slayer II, then Vorkath:** the head is a 1/50 drop, guaranteed on kill 50. Give Ava the head, 75 mithril arrows and the accumulator or 4,999 coins. You need 70 Ranged to wear the assembler.
4. **Late game:** take Dizana's quiver from the Fortis Colosseum to Ava with your assembler so the quiver saves ammo at the 80% rate.

## The Three Devices Compared

All three sit in the cape slot. About 20% of shots always break on impact; the devices differ in how much of the rest they catch.

| Device | Ranged level | Caught | Dropped on floor | Shots per arrow | Ranged attack | Ranged Strength | Defence | What Ava wants |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ava's attractor | 30 | 60% | 20% | 2.5 | +2 | +0 | +2 Magic | Quest reward; 999 coins for a replacement |
| Ava's accumulator | 50 | 72% | 8% | 3.57 | +4 | +0 | +1 slash, +4 Magic | 75 steel arrows plus the attractor or 999 coins |
| Ava's assembler | 70 | 80% | 0% | 5 | +8 | +2 | +1 stab, slash and crush, +8 Magic, +2 Ranged | Vorkath's head, 75 mithril arrows, plus the accumulator or 4,999 coins |

"Shots per arrow" is the wiki's average number of times one piece of ammo is fired before you lose it, assuming you never pick up what lands on the floor. The wiki rates the assembler 1.4 times as efficient as the accumulator, and points out that an attractor user who picks up their dropped arrows reaches the same 80% as an assembler, at the cost of clicking.

The assembler has the second-best Ranged attack bonus of any cape-slot item and is one of the few capes with Ranged Strength. Only Dizana's quiver and its blessed version beat it.

### What they save, and what they don't

Per the wiki, the devices recover arrows, bolts, darts, javelins, throwing knives, thrownaxes and toktz-xil-ul. The wiki's ironman Ranged guide adds that they collect atlatl darts from the Eclipse atlatl. They do **not** save:

- Ammo that is always used up: chinchompas and holy water.
- Weapon charges: Zulrah's scales in the toxic blowpipe, or revenant ether in the webweaver bow.
- Karil's bolt racks, which the ironman Ranged guide notes get no benefit.
- Cannonballs fired from a boat. That is the ballistic attractor's job (see the 2026 changes below).
- Anything at all on free-to-play worlds.

About every 3.5 minutes, if you have moved at least three tiles since the last pickup, each device also "attracts" a piece of metal junk. For the accumulator this is steel arrows 1,975 times in 2,000, and anything it can equip goes into your equipment before your inventory. The attractor pulls iron and the assembler pulls mithril. Right-click **Commune** on the device to switch the junk off.

### Metal bodies stop it working

The magnet fails when you wear a metal torso. From the wiki's interference table:

| Interferes | Compatible |
| --- | --- |
| Ahrim's robetop | Karil's leathertop, Armadyl chestplate |
| Plain iron, steel, mithril, adamant, black and rune platebodies | Void knight top and elite void top |
| Iron, mithril, adamant, black, rune and dragon chainbodies; dragon platebody | Dragon platebody (g), dragon chainbody (g), bronze and white armour, heraldic (h1 to h5) platebodies |
| Dharok's, Guthan's, Torag's and Verac's tops | Fighter torso, Bandos chestplate, Torva platebody |
| Justiciar chestguard; Saradomin, Guthix and Zamorak platebodies | Obsidian platebody, granite body, Oathplate chest, Inquisitor's hauberk |
|  | Blue Moon, Blood Moon and Eclipse Moon chestplates, Sunfire fanatic cuirass |

Trimmed (t) and gold-trimmed (g) platebodies are split: the iron, steel and mithril versions are compatible, while the adamant, black and rune versions interfere. Most melee bodies an ironman actually trains in, such as the fighter torso, obsidian and Bandos, are compatible. Ahrim's robetop is the one the wiki names in its warning, so watch it in hybrid setups.

### The quest in brief

1. Talk to Ava, behind the bookcase's secret door on the ground floor of Draynor Manor's west wing. She wants two undead chickens.
2. At the undead chicken farm west of the Ectofuntus, run between Alice and her husband Malcolm. Fetch a crone-made amulet from the Old Crone east of the Slayer Tower, then buy two undead chickens from Malcolm for 10 ecto-tokens each.
3. Give Ava the chickens. The witch in the manor swaps your 5 iron bars for a selected iron. Hammer it facing north in the centre of the mine north-east of Rimmington to make a bar magnet.
4. Turael in Burthorpe turns your mithril axe and holy symbol into a blessed axe. Chop an undead tree near the manor for undead twigs.
5. Translate Ava's research notes, then combine the hard leather and polished buttons with her pattern to make the container. Hand it in.

The quest lists no required kills.

## Step 2: Ava's Accumulator at 50 Ranged

Once you have 50 Ranged, right-click Ava in Draynor Manor and choose **Devices**. The upgrade costs 75 steel arrows plus either your attractor or 999 coins. If you bring both, she takes the attractor. Perdu's Lost Property shop also sells the accumulator for 7,000 coins at 50 Ranged, but that is only worth it if you are nowhere near Draynor.

Steel arrows for an ironman:

- **Shops:** Lowe's Archery Emporium in Varrock stocks 1,000 at a base price of 12 coins, and Brian's Archery Supplies in Rimmington stocks 1,500. Daryl's Ranging Surplus in Shayzien and Arcuani's Archery Supplies in Tal Teklan hold 1,000 each. Ava's own shop has only 10.
- **Make them:** steel arrowtips at 35 Smithing (15 per steel bar) and steel arrows at 30 Fletching. Five steel bars and 75 headless arrows cover the upgrade.

If you die with the accumulator unprotected it goes to your grave. If you don't reclaim it within an hour, Ava sells you a new one for 999 coins and 75 steel arrows. You can own several devices at once, and Ava's Devices menu lets you buy more than one.

## Step 4: Vorkath's Head

Vorkath lives on Ungael. Talk to Torfinn on the third dock from the west in Rellekka to get there. Vorkath's head is a tertiary drop from the post-quest version:

- **Rate:** 1/50 per kill, and the wiki confirms it is **always** dropped on your 50th kill. Mod Ash has confirmed the 50th kill skips the roll rather than adding one, so you cannot get two heads on that kill.
- **What that means in practice** (our arithmetic from the 1/50 rate): about 32 kills on average, and roughly a 63% chance of the head dropping before kill 50.
- **Slayer:** Vorkath counts as a blue dragon and a zombie task, so the kills can come while you are on task.
- **Deaths:** the fight is in an instance. If you die, Torfinn returns your items for 100,000 coins, taken from your bank or Death's coffer. An unsafe death before you collect them deletes everything left with him.

The wiki's suggested stats for the ranged method are 85+ Ranged, 75+ Defence and 70+ Prayer for Piety, or 74+ for Rigour. Its top ranged weapon is the dragon hunter crossbow with ruby and diamond dragon bolts (e). Pair it with an anti-dragon shield or dragonfire ward and Protect from Missiles. At minimum, combine a super antifire with Protect from Magic, or a regular antifire with an anti-dragon shield. Watch for his fireball, which kills outright if it lands on you, and the zombified spawn, which hits for up to 60 if it reaches you. See the [boss list](https://ironman.guide/bosses) and [money making](https://ironman.guide/money-making) pages for how Vorkath fits an ironman's account beyond the head.

Don't spend your first head on anything else

Every other use of Vorkath's head consumes it: 1,000 Prayer XP at the Dark Altar (1,560 with Master Reanimation at 90 Magic), stuffing it for your skill hall, the turquoise Slayer helmet (after buying Undead Head for 1,000 Slayer points), the turquoise dragon hunter crossbow (after the elite Combat Achievement tier) and the ranging cape upgrade. Take the first one to Ava.

### Buying the assembler

Bring Ava the head, 75 mithril arrows and either your accumulator or 4,999 coins. If you pay the coins, you keep the accumulator as a spare. The assembler needs 70 Ranged to equip.

Mithril arrows for an ironman:

- **Shops:** Lowe's Archery Emporium in Varrock stocks 800 at a base price of 32 coins. Brian's Archery Supplies in Rimmington, Daryl's Ranging Surplus in Shayzien, Arcuani's Archery Supplies in Tal Teklan, Dargaud's in the Ranging Guild, the Quality Weapons Shop in Keldagrim and Sian's Ranged Weaponry in Prifddinas also sell them.
- **Make them:** mithril arrowtips at 55 Smithing (15 per mithril bar) and mithril arrows at 45 Fletching. Five mithril bars cover the upgrade.

The assembler never drops ammo on the floor: 80% comes back automatically and the other 20% breaks. A Masori crafting kit can turn it into the cosmetic Masori assembler, and you can combine it with a max cape to make the assembler max cape.

Losing an assembler costs another head

Ava and Perdu both want a Vorkath's head before they sell you an assembler. Perdu charges 7,000 coins on top. A PvP death, on a PvP world or at any level of the Wilderness, breaks an unprotected assembler, and Perdu repairs it for 240,000 coins. Since the Trouver rework on 17 June 2026, the assembler, the Masori assembler and both assembler max capes break instead of being deleted, even above level 20 Wilderness.

Ultimate ironmen: how to store it

The wiki gives UIMs two ways to bank the assembler's effect. Make the assembler max cape, which counts as a max cape on the POH cape rack, or use a Vorkath's head on a ranging cape for a storable cape with the assembler's ammo saving. See our [UIM guide](https://ironman.guide/uim) for the rest of the storage tricks.

## The Ranging Cape at 99

The ranging cape gives the accumulator's 72% ammo saving with no Animal Magnetism needed. Use a Vorkath's head on it to raise that to the assembler's 80%. You can't use an assembler instead, and the head is consumed. The upgrade then applies to every ranging cape and max cape you own or buy later. The cape does **not** gain the assembler's +8 Ranged attack or +2 Ranged Strength, and metal bodies interfere with it just as they do with the devices. At 99 you need a second head if you want both an assembler and an upgraded skillcape.

## Dizana's Quiver and the Blessed Quiver

Dizana's quiver is the best-in-slot ranged cape. It is guaranteed for beating Sol Heredit, the twelfth and final wave of the Fortis Colosseum in Varlamore (Children of the Sun required), and needs 75 Ranged to wear.

|  | Ava's assembler | Dizana's quiver (uncharged) | Dizana's quiver (charged) or blessed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ranged level | 70 | 75 | 75 |
| Ranged attack | +8 | +18 | +28 for arrows and bolts |
| Ranged Strength | +2 | +3 | +4 for arrows and bolts |
| Ammo saved | 80% | 80% after the Ava upgrade | 80% after the Ava upgrade |
| Metal body interference | Yes | No (for the Ava effect) | No (for the Ava effect) |
| Extra ammo slot | No | Yes, arrows or bolts | Yes, arrows or bolts |

- **Charging:** sunfire splinters give the quiver Dizana's Sunfire: +10 Ranged accuracy and +1 Ranged Strength on arrows and bolts fired from either ammo slot. It holds up to 20,000 charges, and each shot has a 1/3 chance to use one. Sunfire does nothing for blowpipe darts, chinchompas or the Bow of Faerdhinen.
- **The Ava upgrade:** after Animal Magnetism, bring the quiver and your assembler or accumulator (or their max cape versions) to Ava. She adds that device's ammo saving to the quiver without taking the device, and the effect covers every quiver you own now or later. A ranging cape only gives the quiver the accumulator's rate. **For 80% she has to see an Ava's assembler** , which is why ironmen still need a Vorkath's head even if they plan to go straight to the quiver.
- **Blessing:** use the quiver on a Shrine of Ralos with 150,000 sunfire splinters to make the blessed quiver, which has Dizana's Sunfire permanently and can become Dizana's max cape. Splinters you charge into the quiver count towards the 150,000. The wiki warns that fully uncharging it by hand resets that progress, though running out of charges in normal use does not.
- **Splinter sources:** the Colosseum, hunting sunlight antelope, the Hueycoatl and moon key chests. Minimus also gives 4,000 splinters for each spare quiver.
- **Death in the Colosseum:** the wiki marks it as a dangerous instance. Your grave appears in the lobby, and until you have completed 100 waves the reclaim fee is cut by 75%, up to 125,000 coins. The Colosseum is not on the Hardcore Ironman safe-death list.

## 2025 and 2026 Changes

As of October 2026, neither the three device pages nor the wiki's Ava's device page lists a change to stats or ammo rates in 2025 or 2026. The last change to what they recover was javelins becoming recoverable on 29 May 2024. What changed around them:

- **23 July 2025, Varlamore: The Final Dawn:** Lunami's Axe Shop in Auburnvale opened. At 520 coins it is the easiest mithril axe for Animal Magnetism.
- **6 August 2025:** Perdu applying a Trouver parchment to Dizana's quiver no longer wipes its blessing progress. Earlier, on 5 March 2025, charging the quiver started counting towards the blessing.
- **19 November 2025, Sailing:** small salvage, from shipwrecks at 15 Sailing, gives iron bars at 1/34.42, and the wiki now lists it as an iron bar source for the quest.
- **18 March 2026, Boat Combat Fixes:** Ava now gives a **ballistic attractor schematic** to players who have finished Animal Magnetism and Pandemonium. The ballistic attractor is a boat facility that recovers 60% of the cannonballs your boat's cannons fire. It needs 50 Sailing and 44 Construction, and you build it from 10 mahogany planks, 5,000 water runes, 1,000 law runes, 5 lead bars and 5 steel bars. See the [Sailing guide](https://ironman.guide/articles/osrs-sailing-guide) .
- **17 June 2026, Trouver System Rework:** the assembler, Masori assembler and both assembler max capes now break, rather than being lost, on a Wilderness death above level 20. The locked blessed quiver also gained broken and mangled versions.
- **19 August 2026:** the blessed quiver was renamed from "Blessed dizana's quiver" to "Blessed Dizana's quiver".

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you get Ava's assembler in OSRS?

Complete Dragon Slayer II, kill Vorkath until he drops Vorkath's head, then take the head, 75 mithril arrows and either your Ava's accumulator or 4,999 coins to Ava in Draynor Manor. The head is a 1/50 drop and is guaranteed on your 50th Vorkath kill. You need 70 Ranged to wear the assembler.

### What are the requirements for Ava's assembler?

Completion of Dragon Slayer II (which itself needs Animal Magnetism), one Vorkath's head, 75 mithril arrows, and either Ava's accumulator or 4,999 coins. The assembler needs 70 Ranged to equip. Dragon Slayer II needs 200 quest points, 75 Magic, 70 Smithing, 68 Mining, 62 Crafting, 60 Agility, 60 Thieving, 50 Construction and 50 Hitpoints, none of them boostable.

### How do you get Ava's accumulator as an ironman?

Finish Animal Magnetism with 50 or more Ranged and Ava hands you the accumulator directly. Below 50 you get the attractor instead, and Ava upgrades it once you reach 50 Ranged for 75 steel arrows plus the attractor or 999 coins. Ironmen can buy the steel arrows from Lowe's Archery Emporium in Varrock or Brian's Archery Supplies in Rimmington, or fletch them at 30 Fletching from steel arrowtips smithed at 35 Smithing.

### Is Vorkath's head guaranteed at 50 kills?

Yes. Vorkath's head is a 1/50 tertiary drop, and the OSRS Wiki confirms you always receive it on your 50th Vorkath kill. You cannot get two heads on the 50th kill. On average the first head takes about 32 kills, with roughly a 63% chance of it dropping before the 50th.

### Is Dizana's quiver better than Ava's assembler?

Yes. Dizana's quiver is the best-in-slot ranged cape: +18 Ranged attack and +3 Ranged Strength uncharged, or +28 and +4 for arrows and bolts once charged with sunfire splinters, against the assembler's +8 and +2. It needs 75 Ranged and a full Fortis Colosseum clear. It only saves ammo after Ava upgrades it, and to get the 80% assembler rate you must show her an Ava's assembler, so ironmen still need a Vorkath's head first.

### Do Ava's devices work with metal armour?

No. Metal torso armour interferes with the magnet and stops the ammo saving. That includes Ahrim's robetop, rune and dragon platebodies, and Dharok's, Guthan's, Torag's and Verac's tops. Many bodies that look metallic are compatible, including the fighter torso, Bandos chestplate, Torva platebody, obsidian platebody, Oathplate chest and every void top. The ammo saving that Ava adds to Dizana's quiver ignores metal bodies entirely.

### Where can an ironman get mithril arrows for Ava's assembler?

Lowe's Archery Emporium in Varrock stocks 800 mithril arrows at a base price of 32 coins, and Brian's Archery Supplies in Rimmington, Daryl's Ranging Surplus in Shayzien, Arcuani's Archery Supplies in Tal Teklan and the Ranging Guild all sell them too. To make them yourself, smith mithril arrowtips at 55 Smithing (15 per mithril bar) and fletch them at 45 Fletching.
